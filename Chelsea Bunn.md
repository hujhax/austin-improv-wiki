**Chelsea Bunn** is an improviser and actor based in Austin, Texas. She has been studying improv at [[ColdTowne Theater]] since January 2014 and is a graduate of their conservatory. She currently performs every Wednesday at ColdTowne Theater with her troupe [[Loverboy]]. Chelsea has been nominated as “Rookie of Year” (ColdTowne Theater, 2014) and for “Best Leading Actress in a Comedy” (Broadwayworld Austin, 2013).
