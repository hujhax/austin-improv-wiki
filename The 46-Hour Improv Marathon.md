**The 46-Hour Improv Marathon** is the seventh annual [[Hideout Improv Marathon]]. It took place from June 19-21, 2015.

## Schedule
* Friday 5pm (hour 1): A "teen mixer", where the core cast play with teens from the Hideout's classes.
* Friday 6pm (hour 2): "And They're Off" -- free-form improv with the core cast.
* Friday 7pm (hour 3): *[[Shows/Start Trekkin']]*
* Friday 8pm (hour 4): *[[Shows/The Fancy-Pants Mash-Up]]*
* Friday 9pm (hour 5): "At Home with Katy and Tony"
	* "Two actual, lovely British people welcome the core 8 into their Living Room for this hour."
* Friday 10pm (hour 6): [[Troupes/Parallelogramophonograph]]
* Friday 11pm (hour 7): [[Troupes/The Knuckleball Now]]
* Saturday 12am (hour 8): [[Troupes/Bad Boys]]
* Saturday 1am (hour 9): [[Troupes/Squirrel Buddies]]
* Saturday 2am (hour 10): *[[Shows/The Black Vault]]*
* Saturday 3am (hour 11): *[[Troupes/Golden]]*
* Saturday 4am (hour 12): [[Coldtowne Theater]] presents The Bat.
* Saturday 5am (hour 13): [[Troupes/The Amazon and The Milksop]]
* Saturday 6am (hour 14): *[[Shows/Cochise]]*
* Saturday 7am (hour 15): "The Marathon Alumni Show"
	* "A blast from the past. Marathon Alumni come to support and advise the 2014 participants."
* Saturday 8am (hour 16): [[Empty Promises]]
* Saturday 9am (hour 17): "Building Connections"
	* "Students from the Building Connections improv classes join the Marathoners."
* Saturday 10am (hour 18): *[[Shows/Dubbed Indemnity]]*
* Saturday 11am (hour 19): *[[Shows/Control Issues]]*
* Saturday 12pm (hour 20): *[[Shows/The Devil and Halyn Erickson]]*
* Saturday 1pm (hour 21): *[[Shows/History Under the Influence]]*
* Saturday 2pm (hour 22): [[Troupes/What's the Story, Steve]]
* Saturday 3pm (hour 23): "The Eye of the Storm" (free-form improv from the core cast)
* Saturday 4pm (hour 24): *[[Shows/Charles Dickens Unleashed]]*
* Saturday 5pm (hour 25): Double Marathon
	* "The Austin Runners Club stops by to talk about marathons during the marathon."
* Saturday 6pm (hour 26): [[Troupes/Girls Girls Girls]]
* Saturday 7pm (hour 27): [[Troupes/Confidence Men]]
* Saturday 8pm (hour 28): [[Troupes/The Frank Mills]]
* Saturday 9pm (hour 29): *[[Shows/Scene of the Crime]]*
* Saturday 10pm (hours 30 & 31): *[[Shows/Maestro]]*
* Sunday 12am (hour 32): [[Puppet Improv Project]]
* Sunday 1am (hour 33): *[[Shows/Happily Ever After]]*
* Sunday 2am (hour 34): [[Troupes/Franz & Dave]]
* Sunday 3am (hour 35): [[Troupes/MC Harold]]
* Sunday 4am (hour 36): "The Orphan Is Displeased"
	* "An orphan ([[Performers/Bridget Brewer]]) Skypes into the Marathon to control the action."
* Sunday 5am (hour 37): *[[Shows/Tech Nightmare]]*
* Sunday 6am (hour 38): *[[Shows/Care Bear Stare]]*
	* "The Care Bears are here to bring badly-animated joy to your lives."
* Sunday 7am (hour 39): *[[Shows/Buzz Band]]*
* Sunday 8am (hour 40): [[Troupes/The Escorts]]
* Sunday 9am (hour 41): [[Troupes/Of Mice And Mostly Women]]
* Sunday 10am (hour 42): "Fury Road"
	* "*[[Wikipedia - Mad Max -  Fury Road|Mad Max: Fury Road]]* was a hell of a movie, and this will be a hell of a show."
* Sunday 11am (hour 43): *[[Shows/Spirited]]*
* Sunday 12pm (hour 44): [[Troupes/The Available Cupholders]]
* Sunday 1pm (hour 45): *[[Shows/Fakespeare]]*
* Sunday 2pm (hour 46): "Final Show"
	* The victory lap of the 8 core Marathoners. They're free to do whatever they like... except sleep.
