**The 42-Hour Improv Marathon** was the third annual [[Hideout Improv Marathon]].  It was performed in 2011.

## Core Players
* [[Andy Crouch]]
* [[Jason Vines]]
* [[Jordan T. Maxwell]]

* [[Lisa Jackson]]

* [[Tim Redmond]]
* [[Troy Miller]]
