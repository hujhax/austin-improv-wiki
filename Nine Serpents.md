**Nine Serpents** was an improv duo.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2011 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>Two People, Nine Scenes, Endless Hilarity.  Nine Serpents is a two person improv troupe featuring [[Kristin Henn]] and [[Alex Baia]]. Weird offers, compelling replies. This two man Harold creates high energy, eccentric characters and explores unexpected new worlds. The energy is high and the turns unexpected. You should be a part of this!</blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2011 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>We do a two man Harold. Alex and Kristin have played together in [[Oh, Science!]] (a CT house troupe) for two years and decided to take the loose Harold structure used there to create a more concentrated format. Powerful energy and unexpected offers balanced by the unstoppable yes.</blockquote>
