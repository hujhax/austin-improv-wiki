**Brad Hawkins** is an improv performer who works primarily with [[The Hideout Theatre]] and [[The Institution Theater]].

## History
A native of Cedar Rapids, Iowa, Hawkins moved to Austin in 1999. He became interested in improv after watching a [[Confidence Men]] show in mid-2010. He has taken classes at [[The Hideout Theatre]] and [[The Institution Theater]]. He took his first improv class on June 19, 2010, and performed in his first show on September 17, 2010.
After attending the [[Out of Bounds Comedy Festival]] in 2010, he co-founded [[Three Hot Chicks]]. Since then, he has been a member of the troupes [[The Walk-Ons]], [[Franz & Dave]], [[Who Dack]], [[Epic]], [[¡Zarzamora!]], and [[Your Dad's Friends]], and was a founding member of [[Goat]]. In 2014 he directed his first mainstage show, *[[Trust No One]]*, followed by *[[You Can't Stay Here]]* the following year.
