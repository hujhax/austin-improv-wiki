**Ammon Taylor** is an improv musician.

## Summary
Pianist Ammon Taylor is best known as the current accompanist for [[Girls Girls Girls]], but also accompanies many ongoing shows at [[The Hideout Theatre]].

## Troupes
* [[Girls Girls Girls]] (accompanist)

* *[[A Deed So Dark]]* (Musical director)
