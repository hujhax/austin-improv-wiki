**Fifth Friends** is an improv duo.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2015 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>Sunaina and Heather moved to Austin around the same time and became each other's fifth friend in the city. The Fifth Friends soon became best friends, and a troupe was born. </blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2015 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>We tell stories, probably longform. </blockquote>
