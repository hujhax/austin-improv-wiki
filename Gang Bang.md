**Gang Bang** was a "fast-paced, drunken-frenzy improv troupe."

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2012 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>The audience writes down a phrase that one player silently mouths to another player who hasn't seen the phrase. The lip reader interprets what was said and that becomes the first line of a scene. The first player (the one in the know) tries to find a way to get the lip reader to say the actual phrase, ending the scene!</blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2012 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>Explained in blurb. However, unlike most of Gang Bang's shows, this one will not be done very drunk.</blockquote>
