**Hell's Bells** was an improv duo.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2011 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>Jason Vines and Ann Wilson are two performers with no fear of taking giant risks on stage. Each show is a new exciting adventure. </blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2011 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>We do character driven harolds. </blockquote>
