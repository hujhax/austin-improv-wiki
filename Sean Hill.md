**Sean Hill** is an improv performer, director, and teacher.  He is the founder of [[The Hideout Theatre]], and ran the theater until 2009.

## History
A post from him about the first improv show he ever saw:<blockquote>First improv I ever saw was *[[ComedySportz]]* at their location on 6th St in Austin. Late 80's.
</blockquote>
