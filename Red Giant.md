**Red Giant** was an improv troupe associated with [[ColdTowne Theater]].

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2011 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>Red Giant is [[Josh Krilov]], [[Andy Petruzzo]], [[David Hess]], [[Julia Kirchen]], [[Stephanie Russo]], [[Teresa Kubos York]], [[Chris Baldenhofer]], [[Kyle Sweeney]]. The super group focuses on heightening and exploring three base scenes and worlds which are then augmented with additional beats/pop outs to help further dissect their own ideas, notions and creations. A large physical presence helps infuse the shows with big energy and a genuine sense of improv play. DO NOT MISS THE RED GIANT!!!</blockquote>
