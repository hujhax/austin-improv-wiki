**Northward** is an improv duo that focuses on big, distinct characters and strong relationships.

## History
After playing together in *[[Camp Madeupponathaspotta]]*, [[Norman Tran|Norman]] and [[Thedward Blevins|Thedward]] decided to 
continue playing together as a duo. They had their debut show on Wednesday, July 2nd 2014 as part of *[[The Cagematch]]* 
in the July Duo Exhibition round at [[ColdTowne Theater]].
