**Bear Derby** is a [[ColdTowne Student Troupe]].

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2014 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>Bear Derby Coldtowne's newest Student Troupe performs Close Quarters whenever and wherever they get the opportunity. So far they've found themselves in a Palace, a nudist colony, a bowling alley on wheels, your step-dad's basement, and oh so many other wonderful (often sexual (thanks to our audience) places!</blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2013 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>Format is a more open Close Quarters, no focus on using specific parts of the stage to represent locations, but scenework and characters to achieve that. Mostly grounded scenes.</blockquote>
