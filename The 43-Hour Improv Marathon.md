**The 43-Hour Improv Marathon** was the fourth annual [[Hideout Improv Marathon]]. It was performed from 5pm on June 1st to 12pm June 3rd 2012.

![[HardCore4.jpg|The "Hardcore Four", who stayed for the whole marathon.]]
