***You Think You're So Smart*** is an improvised panel show in the style of British programs like *[[Wikipedia - QI|QI]]*, *[[Wikipedia - Never Mind the Buzzcocks|Never Mind the Buzzcocks]]*, and *[[Wikipedia - Would I Lie to You (TV series)|Would I Lie to You?]]*

## Format
In every performance, the panelists discuss facts and trivia in that week's category, provided by the show's host, [[Justin Davis]].

Recurring features include the "Ranter's Corner", where a guest speaker delivers a rant related to the night's topic, and "Spin the Wiki", where Justin pick uses [http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special:Random Wikipedia's "Random article" link] to supply a random topic, and "Would You Rather", which is the classic scneario question where you're given a choice between two alternatives, each with their pros and cons.
