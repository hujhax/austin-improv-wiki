**Two Minute Brother** is an improv troupe associated with [[ColdTowne Theater]].

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2014 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>ColdTowne super-troupe Two-Minute Brother explores a single idea throughout their hilariously playful and physical shows. </blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2014 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>Two-Minute Brother explores the "Modern Harold," a long-form style of improv developed by Chicago improvisers during which improvisers explore a single train of thought throughout the show without adhering to the strict structure of the original Harold. </blockquote>
