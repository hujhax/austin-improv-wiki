**The Institution Theater Awards** are the annual awards given out by [[Theatres/The Institution Theater|The Institution Theater]] for excellence in performance, filmmaking, and contributions to the Institution community.

![[File/TylerAward.jpg.md|[Tyler Reece Booker](Tyler%20Reece%20Booker) accepts a TITie at [The 2013 Institution Theater Awards](The%202013%20Institution%20Theater%20Awards)]]

## Summary
The awards, colloquially known as the "TITies", were first given out in 2013.  Lists of nominees are put together by the faculty and management of The Institution, and then ballots are available to the general public to vote on online.  This culminates in a gala, red-carpet awards ceremony put on by the theater.
