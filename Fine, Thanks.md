**Fine, Thanks** was an improv troupe.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2011 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>Alex, Amy, Bradley, Keegan are Fine, Thanks. Our format is never to use the same format twice. No matter what we are doing our passion for Improv and a strong sense of play insure a good time be had by all. We plan to rock your world and many others.</blockquote>
