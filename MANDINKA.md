Mandinka is Mia Iseman and Andrew Buck. After being thrown together as inaugural members of The Seven Eight Sevens, they decided to form their own duo troupe in late 2012. Their debut show was on December 6, 2012, as part of the Thursday Threefer at The Hideout Theater. Since then, Mandinka has performed at Salvage Vanguard, The Institution, and at Coldtowne Theater (as part of the inaugural Duo Cagematch tournament). 

The pair perform character-driven "mono-pop" shows with a focus on pacing, heightening, and strong character relationships.
