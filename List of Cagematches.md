This **List of Cagematches** lists all the known performances of *[[The Cagematch]]*, a bracketed improv tournament that runs every two months at [[ColdTowne Theater]].

Winners of each match are listed **in bold**.

## 11/4/05-5/4/07 ("King of the Hill" Cagematches)
Through this stretch of time, *The Cagematch* was not a bracketed tournament.  Instead, it was run "King of the Hill" style, with the winner from one week's show coming back for the next.
