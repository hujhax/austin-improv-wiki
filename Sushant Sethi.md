**Sushant Sethi** is an improv performer. He is graduate of the program at [[The Hideout Theatre]] where he is a Teaching Assistant and also takes classes at [[The Institution Theater]]. Sethi has studied with renowned instructors from all over such as: Hill/Jones, Rebecca Drysdale, Lyndsay Hailey, Vinny Francois, Asaf Ronen, and many more. 

"To sit in solemn silence in a dull, dark, dock. In a pestilential prison, with a life-long lock. Awaiting the sensation of a short, sharp, shock. From a cheap and chippy chopper on a big black block."
