**Sushant Sethi** is an improv performer. He is graduate of the program at [[The Hideout Theatre]] where he is a Teaching Assistant. He also takes classes at [[The Institution Theater]] where he is part of the creative team and directing an upcoming show: *[[Happy Fun Time]]*. 

Sethi has studied with renowned instructors from all over such as: [[Asaf Ronen]], Hill/Jones, Rebecca Drysdale, Lyndsay Hailey, Vinny Francois, and many more.
