**DA Foundry** was a musical improv troupe.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2013 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote><p>DA Foundry performs improvised theater with improvised music and songs.</p> 

<p>This dynamic troupe creates rich fictional worlds, and populates them with strong characters, background music, and improvised singing.</p> 

<p>Also, quite funny.</p></blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2012 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote><p>We do improv, musical improv, plays, and films under the DA Foundry umbrella.</p> 

<p>Topping and Susannah also teach Singing Improv, Creative Writing, and coach writers and improvisers.</p> 

<p>The 3rd week of each month DA Foundry has an open rehearsal where we offer a free singing improv class and musical improv workout.</p> 

<p>Some DA Foundry projects involve casting additional improvisers and actors (as for Frontera Fest sketch show).</p> 

<p>We also have plans to travel to other national and international improv festivals.</p></blockquote>
