**Bless This Mess** was a one-off *[[Cagematch]]* team.

## History
They won the "Frank Herbert's Dune" Cagematch in spring 2010, and followed it up with 5/8/10 and 6/12/10 performances in the Cagematch "Tournament of Champions".
