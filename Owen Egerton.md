In addition to performing with ComedySportz from 1995-2012, is a founding member of Master Pancake Theater (originally called Mr. Sinus Theater), a screenwriter and script doctor, and a popular Austin author.  In fact, in 2012, Owen won the Austin Chronicle's Reader's poll for *Best Local Author/Poet*.

Owen's books include: *Marshall Hollenzer is Driving* (2000), *How Best to Avoid Dying* (2007), *The Book of Harold: The Illegitimate Son of God* (2010), and *Everyone Says That at the End of the World* (2013).  

He is happily married to Jodi Egerton, and they have two lovely children.
