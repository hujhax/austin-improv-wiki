**Bolik Not ZIB** was an improv troupe.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2011 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>Bolik Not ZIB are the best/worst roommates on earth and they always find a reason to party! They celebrate anything from Wear Purple for Peace day to Inventors Day in Argentina. Tiny whales, giant cheese wheels, evil monkeys, and the inventor of the hairdryer have all been to the BNZ improvised party blowouts. </blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2011 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>BNZers are having another house party (we find an acutal-ish holiday to celebrate that's real), and each improviser gets their own suggestions (i.e., agenda for the party) and then it's a long form show about the party and it's repercussions.</blockquote>
