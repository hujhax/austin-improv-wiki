
**Topping Haggerty Experience** is the one-person improvised long form show performed by Topping Haggerty.

## History
The Topping Haggerty Experience ("THE") has had many different formats. Topping develops 5 to 6 disparate characters and slowly knits their lives together, and show how they effect each other, and alter the universe, in a show that has been as short as 30 minutes and as long as 50 minutes.

THE was first performed in Buenos Aires, Argentina, as 'CamelBack', and has had many unique formats since then.

THE: CamelBack (Unique jobs in ordinary circumstances)
THE: I want to be a Pony (Ordinary people in extraordinary circumstances)
THE: WisperSmack (Anthropomorphic magical realism)
THE: Today is Not Tomorrow (improvised 007 movie)
THE: Bring It (Audience edits everything - thanks Finland!)


The show has been seen in Finland (2013), Austin (several times between 2002-present), Boston (2010), Toronto, Miami, Chicago (2002), New York City (many times from 2002-2008), Atlanta, Houston (2012), and Buenos Aires (2002).
