a co-production with the Puppet Improv Project and Gnap Theater Projects

"Crack!" debuted Thanksgiving weekend, 2009, and returned to SVT for a run of 6 shows in August, 2010. The show is created by mixing highly volatile puppet characters with a band of uninhibited improvisers. The result is a hilarious variety of songs, scenes in Act 1, and a completely improvise musical in Act 2 -- all conceived and performed on the spot and starring puppets. This is not your average puppet show, and it is definitely NOT a show for young children.
