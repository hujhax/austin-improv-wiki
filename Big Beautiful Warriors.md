**Big Beautiful Warriors** (full name: "Big Beautiful Warlock Presents: Big Beautiful Warriors") is a monthly late-night show that is structured as a traditional game show, but with very non-traditional games.

Created, produced and performed by [[Big Beautiful Warlock]], the show is best known for "Frank Calls," improvised group prank calls.
