## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2011 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>Hellcats features Katie Sicking and Jared Stepp as they fearlessly weavy a two person long form improv show into a rich tapestry right before your eyes. 

Hellcats! Sink your teeth in!</blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2010 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>Two person long form improv - with pop outs and or cut scenes</blockquote>
