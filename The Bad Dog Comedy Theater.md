**The Bad Dog Comedy Theater** was one of several Improv theaters hosting shows during Austin's Improv [[History of Austin Improv#The_Boom_and_the_Bust|"Boom"]] period of the mid-to-late 1990's. It was founded and owned by Marc Pruter and Jon Wiley (of [[Troupes/Monks Night Out|Monks Night Out]] fame,) and Anna Bartkowski.
