**Curtis Luciani** ([[Wikipedia - Help - IPA for English#Key|/ˈluːtʃiːˌɑːniː/]]) is an improv performer and director.

## History
Curtis was a core performer in both [[The 40-Hour Improv Marathon]] and [[The 41-Hour Improv Marathon]].
