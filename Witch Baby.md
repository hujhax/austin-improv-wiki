**Witch Baby** was an improv student troupe from [[The New Movement]].

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2011 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>Witch Baby is a house troupe formed out of students of [[The New Movement]]. We're party animals with hearts of gold. We're sexy office supplies just trying to make our way through the corporate world. We are what you want us to be.</blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2011 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>We perform a montage and sweep edit.</blockquote>
