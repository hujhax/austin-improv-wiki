**The Austin Improv Podcast** is a podcast of peformed improv.

## Summary
The Austin Improv Podcast was begun in December 2012. It is one of the only podcasts in the world composed of performed improv (as opposed to be being about improv). The AIP has showcased a wide variety of Austin's improv talent by featuring a different cast every episode. It's released weekly and is available for free download on iTunes. 

Most, but not all, of the episodes are a Living Room Bat: cast members tell true stories from their lives, then improvise audio scenes inspired by those stories. 

The podcast is hosted and produced by [[Scott Hearne]] and [[Caeriel Crestin]].
