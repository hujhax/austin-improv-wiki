## History
The troupe was part of ColdTowne's sixth graduating class (as was [[Wicked City]]).

They are listed on the *[[Cagematch]]* 2x4 as winning a tournament on 2/28/09, but this seems to contradict the show's bracketing records.

They returned to play in the Cagematch "Tournament of Champions" on 5/15/10.

They also played in [[The 2009 Out of Bounds Comedy Festival]].
