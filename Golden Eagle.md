**Golden Eagle** was a one-off *[[Cagematch]]* team.

## History
They are listed on the *[[Cagematch]]* 2x4 as winning a tournament on 2/28/09, but this seems to contradict the show's bracketing records.  They returned to play in the Cagematch "Tournament of Champions" on 5/15/10.
