***Home for the Holidays*** (original title: ***I'll Be Home for Christmas***) will be the December 2015 [[Lists/List of Hideout Mainstage Shows in Chronological Order#Student_Mainstage_Shows|student mainstage production]] at [[Theatres/The Hideout Theatre|The Hideout Theatre]].

## Summary
### Publicity Blurb
From the show announcement:
> "In *I'll Be Home for Christmas*, the holidays are happening whether you make it home or not.  This is the classic story of someone trying to make it home for the holidays, and all the wacky misadventures and interesting characters they meet along the way."
