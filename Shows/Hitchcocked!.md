***Hitchcocked!*** was a mainstage show at [[Theatres/The Hideout Theatre|The Hideout Theatre]] inspired by the works of Alfred Hitchcock.

## Summary
Each performance included "sceneographers" to re-create cinematic effects onstage.

### Guest Performers
The show included several guest performers who weren't part of the main cast:
* [[Performers/Ace Manning|Ace Manning]]
* [[Performers/Craig Kotfas|Craig Kotfas]]
* [[Performers/Lauren Buck|Lauren Buck]]
