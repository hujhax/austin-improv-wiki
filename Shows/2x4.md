***2x4*** is a performance slot at [[Theatres/The Hideout Theater|The Hideout Theater]] for duos.

## Summary
Every performance of *2x4* features four duos.  It is part of *[[Shows/The Wheel|The Wheel]]*.

## History
The show originally ran Saturdays at 6:30pm in April of 2011.
*2x4* was introduced to replace *[[Shows/The TV Set|The TV Set]]*, a TV-themed improv show that had begun to run out of TV-themed troupes to invite.
