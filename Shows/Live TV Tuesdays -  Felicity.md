***Live TV Tuesdays: Felicity*** refers to the *[[Shows/Live TV Tuesdays|Live TV Tuesdays]]* presentation of the *Felicity* episode ["Gimme an O!"](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0578679/).

## Summary
This show was referred to as "*Teen Angst Tuesdays, Featuring *Felicity".

It played exclusively at [[Theatres/The Highball|The Highball]].
