***The Jukebox Musical Project*** is a series of original comedy mini-musicals at [[Theatres/The Institution Theater|The Institution Theater]]. 

## Summary
Each show is written by [[Performers/Courtney Hopkin|Courtney Hopkin]] and leverages a different music artist's songs as well as time/location for each show. 

The individual musicals run for two performances each, every other month.

## Shows
### The Black Death
Set during The Black Plague and featuring music by the Smashing Pumpkins (March 2015)
#### Cast

* Emily Cawood
