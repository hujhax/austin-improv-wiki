Michael Ferstenfeld is a freelance improv performer, director, and producer.  He's also the co-founder and artistic director of scripted theatre troupe, [http://dystheatre.com Dystheatre], co-creator of [[Http - --wiki.exquisitezombies.com The Exquisite Zombies]], an [http://www.ferstenfeld.com/zombies/mediawiki/index.php5?title=E-learning online course developer], and a TA at [[Merlin Works]].  He's taught a an elective at [[Hideout Theater]] and will be teaching an ACTING/IMPROV [http://www.theinstitutiontheater.com/workshops/41 workshop] at [[Institution Theater]] in April!
