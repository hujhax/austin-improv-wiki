***Puppet Fever*** was an improvised puppet musical.

## History
This show opened *[[Shows/The Saturday Night Special|The Saturday Night Special]]* in June 2011, and then played a performance at [The Twin Cities Improv Festival](http://twincitiesimprovfestival.wordpress.com/) that same year.
