**The Merlin Works Institute for Improvisation** is the improv production and teaching company run by [[Performers/Shana Merlin|Shana Merlin]]. The company was founded in 2003, adopting its current name in 2008.

Merlin Works was based at [[Theatres/Salvage Vanguard Theater|Salvage Vanguard Theater]] and closely affiliated with [[Gnap! Theater Projects]] until 2012, when it moved operations to the ZACH Theatre.

Merlin Works offers six levels of classes, with graduation shows following levels 3 and 6. It also features a faculty troupe, [[Troupes/The Known Wizards|The Known Wizards]]. Merlin Works has a monthly show, called [[Shows/Merlin Works Second Sundays|Merlin Works Second Sundays]], which features a combination of student showcases, guest troupes, and performances by The Known Wizards.
