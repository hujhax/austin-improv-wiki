**The Halls of Power** was an improv troupe.

## Summary
In each show, the troupe would explore an institution and the people who controlled it.  The format had the cast taking an elevator to different floors of a business to see different aspects of a company.

### Pitch
Drew's original pitch:<blockquote>The format is called 'The Halls of Power' and I think I've pitched it to everyone. The idea is that we will get a suggestion of an institution (breakfast cereal, the IRS, whatever) and build a story about the people at the top of that organization. Think Aaron Sorkin shows or *Dallas*. I've always been interested by people with lots of power and how they use it and deal with their peers and 'subjects'. I think it'll be really fun to play that. I don't have anything else as far as structure, so if you're wanting to try something new, feel free to pitch ideas in!</blockquote>

## History
The troupe played Cagematches on 12/13/07 and 1/12/08.
