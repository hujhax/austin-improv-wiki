**Ghost Prom** was the first house troupe at [[The New Movement]].

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2011 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>Ghost Prom was formed in January 2010 as the New Movement Theater's first casted house troupe. Ghost Prom hosts monthly shows showcasing their fellow improv troupes; after each show is a prom with punch and a DJ. We are: Amy Pacheco, James Patrick Robinson, Jennifer Monsees, Dan Rumney, Chad Smith, Milo Smith, and Dave Ward.</blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2011 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>We host themed proms and our sets often revolve around the them of our event. We have an evolving format that utilizes tap-outs, split scenes with an eye on connections either thru place or time. </blockquote>
