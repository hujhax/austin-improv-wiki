***Nothing and Everything*** is an upcoming mainstage show at [[The Hideout Theatre]] inspired by the works of [[Wikipedia - Anton Chekhov|Anton Chekhov]].

## Preview Description
Anton Chekhov is arguably the most celebrated post-Shakespearean playwright.  Original improvised plays will explore new Chekhovian worlds where nothing happens: except love, life, and death.
