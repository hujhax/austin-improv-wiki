**Dave Buckman** is an improv performer, director, and teacher.

## History
Dave moved to Austin in January 2005.

## Summary
A bio from the website for [[Rachel and Dave]]:<blockquote>Dave studied, taught and directed improv in Chicago and served as the Artistic Director of Boom Chicago in Amsterdam. He has performed and taught at comedy festivals around the globe. After Europe, Dave worked as a director and teacher at The Second City in Chicago and Cleveland.</blockquote>
