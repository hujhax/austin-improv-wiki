**The Darkness** was an improv troupe that performed the Bat.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2013 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>
<p>The Darkness is a collaboration between students and alumni from [[The New Movement]] and Hideout Theatres, which began after each theatre had a bat workshop held by [[Justin Strackany]] from The New Movement. </p>
<p>And what is the bat? </p>
<p>The bat is improv in the dark. </p>
<p>No lights. </p>
<p>Just several people seated on stage telling a story through primal sounds, phrases, singular words and emotional conduct.</p>
</blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2013 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>
<p>We do the bat. </p>
<p>Our performance is in the dark. </p>
<p>Sometimes with ambient sound. </p>
<p>Sometimes with nothing but our voices and human harmonics to create a story, a "narrative," organically through phrases, single words and primal sounds. </p>
<p>We're an offspring of a Joe Bill workshop that prompted two similar workshops at both the Hideout and New Movement. </p>
<p>Students from both workshops have shown a strong interest in doing more bat improv in and around Austin</p>so we're looking at theatres to play at.</p>
</blockquote>
