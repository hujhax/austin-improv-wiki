**The Spectacle** is a performance slot at [[The Hideout Theatre]].

## Summary
The Spectacle happens every Friday at 10pm at [[The Hideout]].  It is a double bill with an opening act and then [[Parallelogramophonograph]].  Each act performs for 35-40 minutes.

The acts are scheduled by [[Roy Janik]], who puts out a call for applications every two months.

## History
The Spectacle began in May 2010 as a replacement for [[The Double Barrel]].
