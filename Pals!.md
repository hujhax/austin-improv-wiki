**Pals!** is an improv duo.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2015 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>It seems odd to call a duo a "supertroupe", but there isn't really any other way to describe "Pals!". [[Midnight Society]]'s [[Lance Gilstrap]] and [[Patio Talk]]'s [[Kasey Borger]] are undeniably Pals and they won't stop (comedically) shoving it in your face.</blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2015 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>We perform the "WeirDass" format created by Stephnie Weir and Bob Dassie. It starts with an in-character interview and this informs a run of scenes and a return to the interview.</blockquote>
