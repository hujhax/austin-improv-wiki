**Ben Masten** is an improv performer and transplant from New York City.

## History
Ben trained in improv in New York starting in 2004, first taking classes at UCB, then joining the community at the PIT, where he performed as part of various house teams until his departure. He first visited Austin for [[The Out of Bounds Comedy Festival]] in 2009, coming back every summer after that. He finally moved to ATX full time in 2012.
