**Malk** was an improv duo.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2012 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote><p>Malk is the two person troupe mashing up [[Lance Gilstrap]] and [[Mac Blake]].</p> 

<p>Between them, half of Austin's comedy has been covered... [[Midnight Society]], KOOP's Mascot Wedding, Master Pancake Theater, *[[The Hustle Show]]*, [[Stag Comedy]], [[Hot Property]], and a finalist in the Funniest Person in Austin.</p></blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2013 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>In the past we've done shows based on a topic one of us knows a lot about, and then other is a novice. We've also just taken a suggestion, talked about what we think of with that and then dive right in.</blockquote>
