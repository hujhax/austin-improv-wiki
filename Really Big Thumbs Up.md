**Really Big Thumbs Up** is an improv troupe.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2015 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>Really Big Thumbs Up is a new troupe composed of experienced improvisers who simply love to performing together. Each member has their own style and energy that complement each other beautifully, producing shows that are well-balanced and truly satisfying to watch. Their format that they call "The Close Neighborhood" takes the audience through the connections, relationships, and events that occur between members of a community over a short period of time. You will see how each character's life is intertwined with the others, and how each action affects not only that scene, but shapes the neighborhood as a whole. </blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2015 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>We do a modified close quarters that focuses on how a neighborhood, or community of people's lives evolve and connect over a short period of time. </blockquote>
