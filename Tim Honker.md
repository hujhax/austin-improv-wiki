**Tim Honker** is an improviser and sketch writer/producer/actor/instructor in Austin, Texas and has been performing comedy in Austin and around the country since 2008. 

## Comedy Education and Performances
Tim started taking improv and sketch classes at [[ColdTowne Conservatory]] in 2008 and graduated in March 2009. Tim currently performs improv with [[Nice Astronaut]].  He also co-created the sketch comedy troupe [[Townsville]] with [[Nicole McCracken]].  Tim teaches improv and sketch through Nice Astronaut. Tim co-developed and directed Arthur Simone’s (Best Actor – Austin Chronicle, 2009) one-man sketch show [[Dear Frailty]]. He also wrote and performed with the sketch group [[UpTowne]] and has performed with the [[ColdTowne Touring Company]]. Tim is a co-curator of ColdTowne Theater's [[Bad Movie Night]] along with [[Drew Wesely]]. 

## Non-comedy
Tim graduated from the University of Texas in 2006 with a B.S. in Aerospace Engineering and a minor in Computer Science.  Tim spent several years working in the defense industry as a systems, mechanical, and software engineer. Tim assisted on design of FASTRAC, the University of Texas's nanosatellite, which was launched into low Earth orbit on November 19, 2010.  Tim is also a homebrewer, a NAUI certified Master Scuba Diver, and is credited as working on a few published PC and console video game titles.
