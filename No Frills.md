**No Frills** is a troupe that focuses on monoscenes about women who like each other.

## History
After rehearsing together at [[The Institution Theater]] in the summer of 2013, they had a soft debut at the Institution's *[[Rubber Room]]* on 7/7/13, and then had their official debut at [[ColdTowne Theater]] on 9/2/13 (taking the suggestion "Laundromat").
