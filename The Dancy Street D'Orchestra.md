The Dancy Street D'Orchestra was a narrative musical improv troupe that formed from students of Shana Merlin's first improvised-singing classes.

## History
The troupe was founded in 2010 by Susannah Raulino and Amy Averett, and disbanded after their [[Out of Bounds]] performance in 2011.
