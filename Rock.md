*Rock* was an improvised longform narrative show inspired by rock operas.  All dialog was sung, and punctuated by rock'n'roll musical numbers.  Musician [[Michael Brockman]] improvised accompaniment using two keyboards, a drum machine, and an electric guitar.

*Rock* was a mainstage show at [[The Hideout Theatre]].
