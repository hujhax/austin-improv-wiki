**The 2012 Ladies Are Funny Festival** was the sixth annual [[Ladies Are Funny Festival]].

## Summary
The festival ran from 5/9/12 to 5/12/12 at [[Salvage Vanguard Theater]] and (on its first night) the Spiderhouse Ballroom.
