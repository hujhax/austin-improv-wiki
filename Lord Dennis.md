**Lord Dennis** is an improv duo.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2014 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>We are a real-life husband and wife improv duo. Our shows follow the absurd and weird adventures of modern families, eighteenth-century butlers, and other characters, often with a literary bent.</blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2012 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>We do a monoscene with cutaways or sometimes a few scenes happening in different places at the same moment in time. We are very inspired by Wes Anderson's film style, so we like to do eccentric/highly specific characters and slow-burn comedy.</blockquote>
