**Rachel Posey Austin** is an improv performer and sound tech improviser. 

## History
Rachel Austin is passionate about storytelling and the written word. By day, she works in public relations at Waggener Edstrom Communications. By night, she scribbles out the stories in her head and studies the art of improv. Rachel first began improvising in the spring of 2013 but has always had a love of performance art and participated in theater from a young age. She studied at The Hideout completing Levels 1-6 and Level B Flat.
