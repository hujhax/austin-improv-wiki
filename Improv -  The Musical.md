***Improv: The Musical*** is an upcoming scripted musical at [[The Institution Theater]].

## Summary
The show is written by [[Tom Booker]] and [[Sarah Marie Curry]], with music by [[Wikipedia - Laura Hall (musician)|Laura Hall]] of *[[Wikipedia - Whose Line Is It Anyway (U.S. TV series)|Whose Line Is It, Anyway?]]*

It will run Fridays and Saturdays at 8pm from 3/27/15 to 4/11/15.

### Press Blurb
<blockquote>"Everything you love about the world of improv, scripted and set to music.  Any suggestions?"</blockquote>
