**The Hideout Text Adventure** (actual title: *The Hideout Theatre Adventures: Quest for the Secret Skull*) is a text-adventure game that takes place in [[The Hideout Theatre]].

## History
The game originated in a facebook discussion[https://www.facebook.com/roydanger/posts/10101478959545090] about setting up a possible text-adventure-themed show at the Hideout, to go along with *[[Pick Your Own Path]]*.  [[Brad Hawkins]] jokingly wrote out a walk-through of a (nonexistent) text-adventure game set at the Hideout, and [[Peter Rogers]] ran with the idea, quickly coding up more and more complicated iterations of a Hideout-themed game in [[Wikipedia - Inform 7#Inform 7|Inform 7]].  Eventually, Peter met with [[Kevin Miller]] and [[Marc Majcher]] to plan out the rest of the game, and quickly finished the coding himself.
