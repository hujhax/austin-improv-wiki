**Local Genius Society** is an improv troupe.

## Formats
Local Genius Society has presented a number of different improv formats.
### Beach Cops
Beach Cops is an improv show inspired by *[[Wikipedia - Baywatch|Baywatch]]*.

## History
The troupe formed from Hideout students in late 2010.
