**Bob Olmstead** is an Improv performer who works primarily with [[The Hideout Theatre]] and [[The Institution Theater]].

## History
Bob completed classes at [[The Hideout Theatre]] with his friend and classmate, [[Paul Normandin]].  Bobby and Paulie were two characters the pair often played while in class.  After completing Level 6, Bob invited Paul to form a duo called "[[Two Guys From Yonkers]]" on October 31, 2011.  

Other classmates included [[Brad Hawkins]], [[Chad Wellington]], [[Katherine Greco]], and [[Cindy Page]]. This core group completed Levels 1 through 5 at [[The Hideout Theatre]].  Paul, Bob, Chad, Kat, and Cindy also completed Level 6 together in 2011.

Bob is a fully out Star Wars nerd, owner/operator of Rogue Sage, gifted public speaker, filmmaker, director and marketing Jedi.  Bob has two dogs, *Wicket and Peanut*.
