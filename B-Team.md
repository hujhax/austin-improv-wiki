**B-Team** is an improv troupe.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2014 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote><p>We are all about exploring the feeling of being 2nd best, the underdog, the feeling of being junior varsity, when you may feel like Luigi, rather than Mario; Chloe, rather than Kim; Bush, rather than Cheney.</p> 

<p>You are the Robin when you wish you were Batman.</p> 

<p>We want to dig up that old story and turn into something that will make you laugh and smile or cringe.</p></blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2014 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote><p>Right now, we are a mix of short form games and montage.</p> 

<p>We want to be heavy on audience participation, and aren't afraid of use of music, dance, singing.</p></blockquote>
