***2x4*** is a performance slot at [[The Hideout Theater]] for duos.

## Summary
Every performance of *2x4* features four duos.  It is part of *[[The Wheel]]*.

## History
*2x4* was introduced to replace *[[The TV Set]]*, a TV-themed improv show that had begun to run out of TV-themed troupes to invite.
