**Joshua Gill** is an improv performer who works primarily with [[ColdTowne Theater]].

## History
In late 2008, Josh moved in with [[Arthur Simone]], co-founder of [[ColdTowne Theater]]. After attending a number of shows, in 2009 he began improv classes at Coldtowne. In his level 1 class, [[Lisa Jackson]] seduced him and subsequently married him. He was cast in [[Oh, Science!]] in the summer of 2009 and has played with them since. He also performs with his wife in [[Galactic]], and also with his wife and John Ratliff in [[Dervish]].
