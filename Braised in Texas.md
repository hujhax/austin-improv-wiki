***Braised in Texas*** was an narrated improv show with set characters, based around an annual barbecue competition in a fictional Texas town.

## Summary
[[John Ratliff]] (as "The Stranger") narrated the show.

## History
[[McNichol & May]] first experimented with the show concept in their slot in [[The 42-Hour Improv Marathon]].

The show had several performances after its run, including [[The 2013 Out of Bounds Comedy Festival]].
