***Live TV Tuesdays: Freaks and Geeks*** refers to the *[[Shows/Live TV Tuesdays]]* presentation of the [[Wikipedia - Freaks and Geeks|*Freaks and Geeks*]] episode [[Wikipedia - List of Freaks and Geeks episodes|"Beers and Weirs"]]

## History
After finishing the run of *Freaks and Geeks*, [[Performers/Katie Thornton]], [[Performers/Lindsey Reeves]], and [[Performers/Wyatt Tall]] would go on to reprise their characters in a couple of one-off performances called "GeekProv".
