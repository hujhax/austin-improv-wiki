**Two Guys from Yonkers** is a duo improv troupe compose of [[Paul Normandin]] and [[Bob Olmstead]].

## History
Paul and Bob completed classes at [[The Hideout Theatre]]. The two often played Paulie and Bobby, two characters with New York accents, in class. After completing Level 6, Bob invited Paul to form the duo on October 31, 2011. 

### Name
The name is entirely based on the accent. Neither of the performers has ever even visited Yonkers, NY.
