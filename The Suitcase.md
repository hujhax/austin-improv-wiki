*The Suitcase* is a mainstage show at [[The Institution Theater]].

It opens with a set of monologs, and then the story splits up, with independent scenes happening in a number of locations in and around [[The Institution Theater]].  Each audience member follows one particular set of performers to one particular location, and thus no audience member gets to see the whole story.  The performers re-convene in the theater for a more traditional narrative-improv section, and the story ends with a secret final act.
