**Short and Sweet** was an improv duo.

## Summary
The duo performed scenes and songs, aiming for a vulnerable, theatrical, emotional tone, but often erring on the side of insanity.

## History
They played and won at least one of two "king of the hill" cagematches in early 2006, and did a reunion show on 7/12/12 at *[[The Birthday Clusterfunk]]*, for Caitlin's birthday.
