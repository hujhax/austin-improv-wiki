***Maestro Raw*** (usually stylized as ***Maestro RAW***) is a student-cast version of *[[Maestro]]*.

## Summary
The show runs on the fourth Friday of each month as part of *[[The Wheel]]*.  It is cast almost exclusively from students of [[The Hideout Theatre]].

## History
The show began in July 2012, with that month's revamp of *[[The Wheel]]* .
