**Huge In Japan** (often written all-caps: **HUGE IN JAPAN**) was an improv troupe.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2013 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>HUGE IN JAPAN whips together every form of inspiration they can get their grubby little paws on to kick off a montage of massive proportions (at least in Japan). Actual performance experience in the Orient not included.</blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2013 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>Montage from whatever moves us to get inspired. Rorsach tests, word association, impassioned monologues, real-life stories. Then we delight the ever living shit out of each other and you can watch if you'd like.</blockquote>
