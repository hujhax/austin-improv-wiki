***Danger!*** (full title: ***Danger! The Improvised Lifetime Movie***) was an improvised longform narrative inspired by [[Wikipedia - Lifetime (TV network)|Lifetime]] movies.

## Summary
The show drew heavily from Lifetime's typical "women in peril" movies such as *[[Wikipedia - The Wives He Forgot|The Wives He Forgot]]* and *[[Wikipedia - Stranger With My Face|Stranger With My Face]]*, with a (frequently clueless) female protagonist, excessively dramatic music, deception, manipulation, anguish, and constant eavesdropping.

## History
The show ran Fridays and Saturdays in February 2012.
