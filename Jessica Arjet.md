**Jessica Arjet** is an improv performer, teacher, and co-owner of [[The Hideout Theatre]].

## Summary
Jessica is the founder and manager of the [[Flying Theater Machine]], Austin's only improv comedy for kids. She is a professional clown (Penelope Foolish).  Jessica is a regular performer in Austin mainstage shows.


## History
Jessica Arjet graduated from the [[Heroes of Comedy]] Improv School in 2003.  Upon graduation, she was invited to join the house improv team.

She was a core cast member of [[The 41-Hour Improv Marathon]], and will be a core cast member in [[The 44-Hour Improv Marathon]].
