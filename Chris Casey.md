Chris is a dad from Austin, TX. He sometimes improvises at [[The Hideout Theatre]] and sometimes acts at The Curtain Theatre with [http://thebaronsmen.org/ The Baron's Men]. Currently he is a member of the improv troupes [[Space Swank]], [[Human People]], and most often [[Too Shy Guys]]. In his spare time, he writes software for IBM.

He was inspired to try improv by his kids who also took classes at [[The Hideout Theatre]]. He won his first Maestro RAW in 2015 and is making plans for his second.
