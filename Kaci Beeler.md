**Kaci Beeler** is an improv performer, director, scenic painter, teacher, and the Director of Design for [[The Hideout Theatre]]. Kaci is a represented actor with TAG Talent Agency[http://www.tagtalent.com/Main.php?Region=Tx&Branch=Main&Entry=Talent&TCode=KB05] who represent actors in film, television, commercial, voice-over, and print media. 

## History
Kaci Beeler began learning and studying improv in 2002 at Westwood High School[http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westwood_High_School_(Austin,_Texas)] in North Austin, Texas, which is a Fine Arts Academy[http://www.roundrockisd.org/index.aspx?page=3530] within the Round Rock Independent School District. In addition to taking a Comedy/Improv course she auditioned and was cast in [[The PITS]] (Premier Improvisational Theater Society) in 2003, an all-student improv troupe led by organizer [[Philip Aulie]]. The PITS went on to become Comedic Amoeba and performed three shows at [[The Hideout Theatre]] in 2004 and 2005. They were coached by [[Jeremy Sweetlamb]] before dissembling in the July of 2005.
