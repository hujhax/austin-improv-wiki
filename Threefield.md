**Threefield** (AKA **John Frum Day**) was an improv troupe. More specifically, it was a subset of [[Parallelogramophonograph]].

## Summary
Threefield was primarily a Cagematch troupe, and its 'format' was "The Fuckaround", which basically means that anything goes, as long as it is fun.

Threefield eventually changed its name to "John Frum Day", though it's unclear if it ever performed under that name.
