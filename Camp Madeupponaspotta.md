***Camp Madeupponathaspotta*** was the April 2014 student mainstage production at [[The Hideout Theatre]].

## Summary
*Camp Madeupponathaspotta* featured improvised coming-of-age stories about the lives of teens & camp counselors at a fictional summer camp. The characters and details were different every show. The show focused on positivity, ensemble, and creating endearing moments as well as hilarious ones.
