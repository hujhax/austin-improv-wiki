**Perfect Teeth ** is an improv troupe.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2015 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>Perfect Teeth puts a spotlight on the quirky, fun, and sometimes outrageous group dynamic. In their unique format "Tableauki", Perfect Teeth shows the evolution of individuals through one bonding group experience. </blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2015 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>We do long form montage improv with a focus on group scenes </blockquote>
