The **Excused Absence Improv Festival for Youth** is an annual festival held at [[ColdTowne Theater]].

It was first held the weekend of May 1 - 3, 2015 where, over the course of three days, youth troupes and adult troupes performed countless shows. Workshops were offered to both youth and youth educators. 

The fourth annual Excused Absence Improv Festival will be held August 11-12, 2018.

[[Kristin Henn]] serves as Managing Director of the festival.
