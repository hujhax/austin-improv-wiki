Alex Walker is an Atlanta transplant who moved to Austin in February of 2015. She began improvising in Atlanta in 2014 and continued her improv education at the Hideout Theatre in 2015 where she graduated later that year from Level 7. She fosters a dog named Shamrock who is a chiweenie with diabetes. She enjoys playing soccer, improvising, and drinking margaritas.
