Mary Henderson took a free improv class at The Hideout in 2012 and was hooked for life. She continued by taking all levels at The Hideout including their first Summer Narrative Intensive in 2014. Along the way, she also picked up several improv levels at Merlin Works including their Singing Improv classes levels 1 – 3. 

Mary has been in 2 troupes, both of which are on hiatus.
