Beaumont native and recent Austin transport, Chris Villafano stumbled across improv in 2011. Eventually, he decided to take classes in 2012. Since then, he has done a variety of shows, jams, mixers, and classes at all 5 theaters. Because of his love for improv, Chris considers himself to be a journeyman in the improv community. As of 2015, Chris is in 3 troupes, Character Explosion, Weekly Liturgy, and Sugar, Water, Purple.
