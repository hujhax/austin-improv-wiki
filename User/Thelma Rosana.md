Thelma Faglie is a born and raised Austinite, who only discovered improv after attending a free workshop at The Institution Theater. She absolutely found her calling and hasn't stopped then. Still fairly new to the Improv community she is determined to get as much stage time as possible, so if she is not performing Improv, you will find her playing the violin and singing in a Mariachi or as Master of Ceremonies for local social events. She has joined the newly founded troupe Candy Vampires who is set to start performing in 2014. Thelma is happily married to a real life Teddy Bear and has a baby bear on the way. Thelma is a coffee snub, chocolate lover with absolutely no remorse, prefers the color blue over all colors and can watch reruns of The Office always.
