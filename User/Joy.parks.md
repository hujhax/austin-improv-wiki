Joy Parks began taking improv classes at the Hideout Theatre in the fall of 2013, and has since studied at The Hideout, The Institution Theatre, and Merlin Works. Favorite credits/involvement in the AIC (so far) include SongRunner (a Hideout Student Mainstage Show) and being a TA for the Hideout's "Building Connections" program for students with special needs.
