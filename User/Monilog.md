Monica first saw improv in San Jose, CA at Comedy Sportz. 25 years later she began taking classes at ColdTowne. She was addicted! She continued taking classes at The Hideout, which she considers her improv home. She has played with the Like Likes and  Pupcakes, formed from the classes she took. In 2015, she invited 8 amazing improvisers to form a troupe. #WIG was born. Monica is also the stylist for The George Family Goats.
