GM and Co-owner ComedySportz Austin.
Shawn Utterback started his improv journey in 1994 with Comedy Sportz in San Jose, CA. Shortly after he relocated to Richmond, VA where he directed Take 5 Comedy Troupe. In 1996 he became a founding member of Comedy Sportz Richmond. Shawn took a hiatus while attending graduate school at VCU to be followed by performances with various improv groups such as Dysfunction Junction, Louisville Improvisors, Project Improv and The Nannie Tharpe Experience. He moved to Austin in 2009 and quickly rejoined his ComedySportz family. Currently residing in Austin, Shawn is the co-founder of Play Storming Productions.
