Linzy Beltran is an improviser who has been in the following troupes: Stone Airplane, That Racket! and A Penny Dreadful. She has taken improv at both Merlin Works and at the Hideout Theater. She currently works at Austin Film Festival as the Executive Assistant. She graduated from The University of Texas at Austin in 2012 with degrees in Broadcast Journalism and Spanish, Hispanic Studies. She currently resides in Austin, Texas.
