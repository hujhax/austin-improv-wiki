Ann graduated from Baylor University with a BA in Theatre Performance in 2005. She moved to Denver in 2006 and became a founding member of Hit & Run: Musical Improv and worked for nearly a decade with Dinner Detective Murder Mystery in both Denver and Boulder. She moved to Austin in 2012 and founded Newsical: Musical Improv with her husband, Austin Terrell, as well as Kim Cox and Jason Finkleman. She began working with the Institution theatre in 2014 with her role in Rock and Roll Purgatory and she began TAing classes shortly thereafter.
