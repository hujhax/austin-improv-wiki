David Rosenbaum is an improv performer and musical improviser. He was a member of the troupes My Friend Stripey and Umami, and musical improviser for The Dancy Street D'Orchestra and Da Foundry.  He is currently a performer and musical improviser for an as yet unnamed musical troupe. He performed in the improv shows Danger! The Improvised Lifetime Movie and Only Three Will Survive, and acted, played guitar, and sang in the sketch shows Akimbo Bubble Scuttle Ruckus, Scrubs Live, and The Moral Compass Rumpus.
