Michelle started taking improv classes with Courtney Hopkin at the Hideout Theater in the summer of 2016. Before doing improvised theater, she studied improvised styles of dance for over a decade and enjoys making things up with movement and words for unsuspecting audiences. Most recently, Michelle has performed in Start Trekkin’ and as a member of the troupe Well Plaid.
