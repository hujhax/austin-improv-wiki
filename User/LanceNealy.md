Lance Nealy is currently a student at ColdTowne Theater in Austin, TX studying improv and sketch comedy.  He Is a member of the improv troupes, After Midnight, Sorry Your Loss, Grounded in Harmony, Donkey Tattoo? and the improv duo, Replacement Mark (with KC Harvey-Taylor). He's a producer and provides tech for the monthly short-form game show, GameTowne.  He was also cast in the ColdTowne main stage shows, Organ Trail (October 2014) and Fifty Nifty (February 2015).  He moved to Austin from New York City in late-2013 and fell in love with improv after the free 101 improv class at ColdTowne Theater.  He's subsequently taken workshops with Dave Buckman, Michael Jastroch and Nice Astronaut.  

In addition to working at ColdTowne Theater, he continues to telecommute for the NYC-based Electus Digital, creators of CollegeHumor.com, Dorkly.com and WatchLOUD.com.  He likes tacos, tater tots and American whiskey.
