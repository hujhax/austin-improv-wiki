***Epic Telescope*** is a longform show.

## Format
There's an improv game called "Half Life", in which the cast performs (say) a two-minute scene, then re-runs it thirty seconds, then fifteen seconds, then perhaps seven seconds.

Epic Telescope reverses and expands this game.  The cast performs (say) a one-minute scene, then stretches it out the same material to three minutes, seven minutes, then perhaps fifteen minutes.

## History
The show began as a one-off for the premiere of *[[The Free Fringe]]* on 4/26/12.

It will be part of [[The 44-Hour Improv Marathon]].
