**Bedridden** was an improv and sketch troupe.

## History
[[Bedridden]] was one of the first troupes of the [[Austin Improv Collective]] era, and one of the first troupes to play an [[Austin Improv Collective]]-branded show.

They performed a sketch show written by [[Peter Rogers]] and [[Ceej Allen]] in [[Frontera Fest]].
