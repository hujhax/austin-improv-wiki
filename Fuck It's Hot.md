***Fuck It's Hot*** was a series of Austin-themed sketch-comedy runs that ran as a mainstage show on Saturday nights at [[The Institution Theater]].

## Summary
The show was directed by [[Tom Booker]] and written by [[Brently Heilbron]]. After the initial writing of the sketches they were further developed through improv.

The show sometimes featured special guest ukulele player [[Ryan Hill]].

## History
The show had several runs with different titles.

### Fuck It's Hot
The initial run ran through July and August of 2013.  It sold out every performance, and was held over for two additional performances in September.  [[Chelley Pyatt]] worked tech for this run.
