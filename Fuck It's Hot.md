***Fuck It's Hot*** was a series of Austin-themed sketch-comedy runs that ran as a mainstage show on Saturday nights at [[The Institution Theater]].

## Summary
The show was directed by [[Tom Booker]] and written by [[Brently Heilbron]]. After the initial writing of the sketches they were further developed through improv.

The show sometimes featured special guest ukulele player [[Ryan Hill]].

## History
The show had several runs with different titles.

### Fuck It's Hot
The initial run ran through July and August of 2013.  It sold out every performance, and was held over for two additional performances in September.  [[Chelley Pyatt]] worked tech for this run.

### Fuck It's Christmas
![[Fuck It's Christmas.jpg|Publicity banner for "Fuck It's Christmas".]]

The show returned for a holiday run in November and December of 2013.  [[Bryan Curry]] worked tech for this run.

#### Cast
* [[Ash Nunley]]
* [[Bryan 'Lubu' Roberts]]
* [[Claudio Fox]]
* [[Duncan Coe]]
* [[Elena Weinberg]]
* [[Sarah Marie Curry]]
* [[Tom Booker]]

#### Sketch List
This was the running order for *Fuck It's Christmas*:
* Opening Number (song)
* Office Party #1 (improv)
* Whole Foods (sketch)
* Craft PSA (improv)
* Tamale Lady (improv performance art)
* Austin Christmas Album (video)
* Office Party #2 (improv)
* Lubu Rap (song)
* 50 Shades of God (sketch)
* Carriage (sketch)
* Office Party #3 (improv)
* 12 Drugs (song & improv)
* Santa Bound (sketch)
* Christmas Of The Future (video)
* Soup Peddler (song)
* Office Party #4 (sketch)
* Closing Number (song)
