***Fuck It's Hot*** was an Austin-themed sketch comedy show produced as a mainstage show at [[The Institution Theater]].

## Summary
The show was directed by [[Tom Booker]] and written by [[Brently Heilbron]]. After the initial writing of the sketches they were further developed through improv. The original run of the show in July and August of 2013 sold out every performance. It was held over for two additional performances in September of that same year. The show was followed by *[[Fuck It's Christmas]]* during the holiday season.

The show sometimes featured special guest ukulele player [[Ryan Hill]].
