***Fuck It's Hot*** was an Austin themed sketch comedy show produced as a mainstage show at [[The Institution Theater]]. It was directed by [[Tom Booker]] and written by [[Brently Heilbron]]. After the initial writing of the sketches they were further developed through improv. The original run of the show in July and August of 2013 sold out every performance. It was held over for two additional performances in September of that same year. The cast is currently working on Fuck, It's Christmas which is set to run during the holiday season.

The show sometimes featured special guest ukelele player [[Ryan Hill]].
