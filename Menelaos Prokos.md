[[Category/Performers|Prokos]]

**Menelaos Prokos** is an improviser who, during his tenure in Austin, was associated primarily with [[The Hideout Theatre]], beginning in 2010. In 2012, he returned to his native Greece, where he opened and runs his own improv school.
