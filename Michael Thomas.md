**Michael Thomas** is an improv performer and videographer.

## History
Michael Thomas got his start in 2008, in the heyday of [[The Tuesday Night Jam]] at [[The Hideout Theatre]].  Eventually, he performed frequently, and became a company member of [[Gnap! Theater Projects]].  He was a frequent videographer for [[Gnap!]], putting together (among other things) the opening sequence for *[[Totally Improvised, 90210]]* and the trailers for *[[Dusk]]*.  He also wrote the music for the songs in *[[Live TV Tuesdays -  Firefly]]*, with [[Asaf Ronen]] writing the lyrics.

In 2012, Michael Thomas moved away to San Francisco.
