**Florida Room** (full name: **Florida Room: Improvised Golden Girls**) was an improv troupe that did shows inspired by the 1980s sitcom *[[Wikipedia - The Golden Girls|The Golden Girls]]*.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2011 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>Florida Room: Improvised Golden Girls creates a completely improvised episode of the show complete with cheesecake and lots of floral. Starring: [[Ashley Nugent]] as Dorothy, [[Leah Moss]] as Sophia, [[Katie Thornton]] as Blanche and [[Emma Holder]] as Rose. </blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2009 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>We improvise an episode of *The Golden Girls* based on an audience suggestion. We eat lots of cheescake during the show and wear costumes. It's a good time!</blockquote>
