***Stool Pigeon*** was a weekly [http://wiki.improvresourcecenter.com/index.php?title=The_Armando_Diaz_Experience,_Theatrical_Movement_and_Hootenanny Armando] at [[ColdTowne Theater]].

## Summary
Every week, the "pigeon" would be a local-celebrity, non-improvisor guest.  The guest would tell stories; the cast would use those as the inspiration for improvised scenes.
