**Madi Goff** is an actress and improv performer.

## History
Madi started improvising in 2008 with [[ComedySportz]].  Shortly afterwards, she started taking classes with [[Shana Merlin]] and [[The Hideout Theatre]].

Over the next few years, Madi performed in longform shows with [[Gnap! Theater Projects]].

In 2011, she moved to Los Angeles.  Since then, she's performed with Impro Theatre's Studio Company in *Improvised Tennessee Williams* and *Improvised Jane Austen.* Madi is now a member of Impro Theatre's Performance Lab. With the Impro LAB she performed in *Hope Falls,* an improvised soap opera and "Willim Inge Unhinged."

She's also performed in LA with iO West's improvised take on *Doctor Who.*
