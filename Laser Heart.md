**Laser Heart** was an improv troupe.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2012 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>Laser Heart is more than two ladies and two dudes doing improv. Laser Heart is a group of four souls linked at Level One. Laser Heart is the trees and the mountains. Laser Heart is forgotten biology assignments. Laser Heart is your diary. And Laser Heart is love. But it is also two ladies and two dudes doing improv. You can 'like' them on Facebook at www.facebook.com/laserheart.</blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2012 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>At first glance, Laser Heart performs a montage, but we've adjusted it and made it more of our own, including incorporating organic transitions, seamless scene edits, etc. We are four people who trust each other enough to have fun and it shows.</blockquote>

## History
The troupe played in the 2011 [[Hell Yes Fest]].
