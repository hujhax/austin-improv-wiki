Salvage Vanguard Theater was, for several years, the home of [[Gnap! Theater Productions]], which used the building's smaller theater space for a number of continuing shows and mainstage runs.

[[Gnap!]] has since folded its improv-production work, and now the theater only sporadically rents out its space to independent improv productions.
