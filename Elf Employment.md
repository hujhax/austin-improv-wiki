***Elf Employment*** is a Christmas-themed improvised puppet musical.

## Summary
This show is based around the elves working in Santa's workshop.  Every week, a different improvisor guest-stars as Santa.

## History
### 2011 Run
In 2011, the show ran Fridays and Saturdays at 8pm at [[Salvage Vanguard Theater]], from 12/2/11 to 12/17/11.
