***The Jam*** was an improv show/mixer at [[ColdTowne Theater]].

## Summary
The Jam was held every Sunday night at 8:30, with the first performance on February 10, 2010. [[Murphy]] hosted it until October 2010, at which point [[Oh, Science!]] took over hosting duties. The show featured a different opening act every week. Audience members were then invited to join Oh, Science! and the opening act on stage for an open jam.

The final Jam was on May 5, 2013. Afterwards, the open jam was dropped, and the 8:30PM Sunday slot became [[Improvised Comedy with Oh, Science!]].
