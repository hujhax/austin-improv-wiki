Quinn Buckner is an improv performer, software engineer, and all around cool guy.

## History
In 2008 while attending UT Quinn saw some *[[Gigglepants]] shows on campus and immediately fell in love with the way someone could get on stage and be bound by nothing more than the imagination. He had to wait till the next year to try out as the auditions had happened the month before. In September of 2009 he auditioned, made it in and met best friends and future troupe mates *[[Colin Bates]] and *[[Kyle Traughber]]. In November he started taking classes at *[[Merlin Works Institute for Improvisation]] and went through 101-601 back to back. While still in 101 via the [[Merlin Works Mixers]] he made it into his first troupe *[[Anticdotes]] coached by *[[Ace Manning]]. In March of 2010 he joined another Merlin Works Mixers troupe *[[]] coached by *[[Topping Haggerty]] and shortly after got invited to be apart of his first show the 2010 run of *[[Guilds of Steel]]*. 2010 was also his first festival as he played in [[The Out of Bounds Comedy Festival]] with Anticdotes.
