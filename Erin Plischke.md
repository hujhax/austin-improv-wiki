**Erin Plischke** was an improv performer who was a cofounder of [[Tight]]. He also performed as part of [[ComedySportz]], where he met longtime partner [[Jen Cargill]]. The two moved to Chicago in 2001, where they performed and studied with iO Chicago. He and Jen returned to Austin in 2004 where he co-founded [[Wooden Nickel]], [[$10 Bourbon]] and [[Tight]]. He and Jen moved to Seattle in 2006, where they perform under the name [https://www.facebook.com/Carskee-117693054945309/ Carskee].
