**Straight Blades** was an improv duo.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2011 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>Straight Blades play improv the old-fashioned way: by paying attention to the scene and letting it tell them where it wants to go. [[Kareem Badr]] ([[Paralllelogramophonograph)]] and [[John Ratliff]] ([[The Glamping Trip]]) know that sometimes the funniest comedy comes from rubbing two straight men together. </blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2011 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>Basically grounded scenework; in rehearsals it's tended toward monoscenes but we're still exploring. </blockquote>
