**The Foreign Legion** was an improv troupe composed entirely of foreign-born improvisers.

## Summary
The Foreign Legion explored truly American customs, locations and concepts by sending two members of the troupe on a quest to find out as much as possible about the history and secrets surrounding the topic. At the end both heroes had to explain their findings to an immigration officer and were either allowed to stay or would get deported, depending on the audience vote.

## History
* 6/18/11 - SVT *[[Saturday Night Special]]* ("Unionized Labor")
* 7/2/11 - SVT *[[Saturday Night Special]]* ("4th of July")
* 7/24/11 - *[[The Weekender]]* @ Hideout ("NRA")
* 7/30/11 - SVT *[[Saturday Night Special]]* ("NASCAR")
* 8/18/11 - *[[The Threefer]]*, 8pm @ Hideout ("Thanksgiving")
* 8/20/11 - SVT *[[Saturday Night Special]]* ("Sea Turtles")
* 9/11/11 - 8.30pm @ Coldtowne ("Baseball")
* 9/14/11 - 8.30pm @ Coldtowne Theater ("Niagara Falls")
* 9/22/11 - 8pm @ Hideout ("Apple Pie")
* 10/29/11 - 10pm @ SVT SNS ("Statue of Liberty")
* 12/4/11 - *[[The Weekender]]* @ Hideout ("Black Friday")
* 1/28/12 - SVT *[[Saturday Night Special]]* ("Greek Fairy Tale: The Foolish Turtle")

After [[Menelaos Prokos]] went back to his home country of Greece, The Foreign Legion stopped performing.
