**Fall Then Smirk** was a one-off *[[Cagematch]]* team.

## Summary
The troupe was set up to be an analog to [[The Frank Mills]] ("Fall Then Smirk" is an anagram for that name), with each cast member playing in the style of one of the [[Frank Mills]] troupe members:
* [[Ann Potter]] as [[Erika May McNichol]]
* [[Brent Foshee]] as [[Todd Schanbacher]]
* [[Brett Tribe]] as [[Dave Buckman]]
* [[Kate Bojanek]] as [[Rachel Madorsky]]
* [[Sam Malcolm]] as [[Bob McNichol]]
** (Sam was unable to make it to the show.)

## History
They played one Cagematch on 5/21/11.
