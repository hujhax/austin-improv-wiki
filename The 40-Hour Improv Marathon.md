**The 40-Hour Improv Marathon** was the first annual [[Hideout Improv Marathon]].  It was performed in 2009.

## Schedule
* Friday 5pm: *[[Shows/Maestro]]*
* Friday 7pm: "Flowey Donut" (free-form improv with the core improvisors)
* Friday 8pm: *[[Shows/Start Trekkin']]*
* Friday 9pm: *[[Shows/TheatreSports]]*
* Friday 10pm: "This Week Tonight", improv inspired by the news
* Friday 11pm: *[[Shows/Cochise]]*
* Friday midnight: "Parlor Games"; twisted games with [[Troupes/Your Terrific Neighbors]]
* Saturday 1am: [[Troupes/Available Cupholders]]
* Saturday 2am: [[The New Movement]]
* Saturday 3am: [[Troupes/Northshore Local]]
* Saturday 4am: [[Troupes/Snackers]]
* Saturday 5am: [[Troupes/Midnight Society]]
* Saturday 6am: [[Troupes/The Starter Kit]]
* Saturday 7am: "The Sleepover", an improvised slumber party with [[Troupes/Look Cookie]].
* Saturday 8am: [[Troupes/No Chaser]]
* Saturday 9am: "Pancake Social", a mimosa party and improv cooking show
* Saturday 10am: *[[Shows/Ka-Baam!!]]*
* Saturday 11am: [[Shows/Charles Dickens Unleashed|"What the Dickens!?", improvised Charles Dickens]]
* Saturday noon: Student Showcase
* Saturday 1pm: [[Troupes/ComedySportz]]
* Saturday 2pm: [[Shows/Flying Theatre Machine]]
* Saturday 3pm: Student Showcase
* Saturday 4pm: "Play by Play: Cubs vs. White Sox", improv commentary over Major League Baseball
* Saturday 5pm: "The Life Game", improv inspired by a live celebrity interview
* Saturday 6pm: *[[Shows/Lola]]*
* Saturday 7pm: *[[Shows/After School Improv]]*
* Saturday 8pm: *[[Shows/Improvised Shakespeare]]*
* Saturday 9pm: [[Shows/Holy 1960s Batman, Batman!|"Holy Sleep Deprivation, Batman!"]], an improvised episode of the 60s *Batman* tv show
* Saturday 10pm: *[[Shows/Maestro|Musical Maestro]]*
* Saturday midnight: *[[Shows/Dubbed Indemnity]]*
* Sunday 1am: [The Bat](http://improvencyclopedia.org/games/The_Bat.html) with [[Troupes/ColdTowne (Troupe)|ColdTowne]]
* Sunday 2am: [[The Institution]]
* Sunday 3am: "Musical [Armando](http://improvencyclopedia.org/games//Armando.html)", with [[Troupes/A Bunch of Liars]] and [[Troupes/The Love Notes]]
* Sunday 4am: [[Troupes/Parallelogramophonograph]]
* Sunday 5am: "Mystery Meat", a surprise format from [[Performers/Roy Janik]]
* Sunday 6am: "Soap Opera", improv daytime television with [[Troupes/Junk]]
* Sunday 7am: [[Troupes/McNichol and May]]
* Sunday 8am: "The Final Countdown", free-form improv with the core improvisors
