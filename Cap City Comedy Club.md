**Cap City Comedy Club** (formerly **The Laff Stop**) is a professional stand-up comedy club in Austin, Texas and home to the Funniest Person in Austin competition. It also played a part in the early history of improv comedy in Austin.

In the 1980s, the venue was known as the Laff Stop. In 1986, [[Troupes/The Laff Staff]] was created from a series of workshops run by actor [[Rodney Rincon]].  The troupe included [[Robert Faires]], [[Dee Llewelyn]], and [[Joel McKean]] (Michael McKean's younger brother). Making their debut in early 1987, they performed a 45-minute pre-show lounge slot four days a week for about five years.
