**Minority Report** is an improv troupe.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2015 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>Everyone has flaws, and instead of hiding them, we'll be flaunting them! Join us as weave a narrative based on the audience's funniest flaws.</blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2015 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>Before the show starts, we'll ask the audience to fill out their funniest flaws on pieces of papers. We will provide paper, pen, and a container and maybe put it on the box office. If there's not enough room, we'll put it on a table, and ask the person working the box office to tell people to fill it out. On stage, we'll each draw a flaw, read it to the audience, and that will inspire the characters we play. We then do a narrative!</blockquote>
