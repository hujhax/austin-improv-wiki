**The Ones** is an improv troupe.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2014 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote><p>The Ones kick it out with naive elements while telling a story.</p> 

<p>Some of us won't know what's going on.</p> 

<p>But you as the audience will always know our secrets.</p></blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2014 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote><p>We do a three scene narrative but some of the players do not see the first scene.</p> 

<p>We tell a story with naive aspects.</p></blockquote>
