**Oh, Science** was founded as a ColdTowne student troupe in July of 2009. They host a weekly show at [[ColdTowne Theater]] followed by [[The Jam with Oh, Science!|an open jam]]. Members include [[Kristin Henn]], [[Alex Baia]], [[Josh Gill]], [[Ed Melendez]] and [[Nathan Livni]].
