**The 2007 Ladies Are Funny Festival** was the first annual [[Ladies Are Funny Festival]].

## Summary
The festival ran from 4/27/07 to 4/28/07 at [[The Hideout Theatre]].

It included workshops, an all-star musical Armando, an all-female, all-star [[Maestro]], and a panel discussion about women in improv.
