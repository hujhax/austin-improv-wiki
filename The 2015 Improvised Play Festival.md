![[IPF 2015.jpg|Publicity image for the festival.]]
**The 2015 Improvised Play Festival** was the fifth annual [[The Improvised Play Festival|Improvised Play Festival]].

It ran from 4/9/15 through 4/12/15.

It was produced by [[Thedward Blevins]] and [[Ruby Willmann]].
