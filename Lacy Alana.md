## History
Prior to 2009, Lacy's only experience with improv included watching Whose Line is it Anyway on TV as a kid, and playing a random selection of improv games in a high-school theatre class.  In 2009, [[Shana Merlin]] guest lectured in one of Lacy's creative advertising classes.  Two years later, after completing graduate school, Lacy finally had time to sign up for classes.   Lacy completed levels 101-601 at [[Merlin Works Institute for Improvisation]].  In her first year of improvising, Lacy also took all of the other classes offered by MerlinWorks including Improv Singing 101-301 and several "improv labs."  Lacy has also taken classes at [[The Hideout Theatre]] and has taken workshops from out of town teachers during specialized workshops and festivals. 

Since starting to improvise, Lacy has been cast in mainstage shows at [[The Hideout Theatre]] ([[Strange Worlds]] and [[TheatreSports (mainstage run)]], [[ColdTowne Theater]] ([[Sci-Fi Saturdays]]), [[The Institution Theater]] ([[Only Three Will Survive]]) and with [[Gnap! Theater Projects]] ([[The Professor -  Improv Inspired by Doctor Who]]).  Lacy has played in [[The Out of Bounds Comedy Festival]], [[The Improvised Play Festival]], and Improviganza (in Hawaii).  Lacy is an active member of [[RhinoDino]] and [[The Known Wizards]], a MerlinWorks teacher troupe.  

Lacy is currently a teaching assistant for Merlin Works adult classes and The Hideout Theatre's teen classes.
