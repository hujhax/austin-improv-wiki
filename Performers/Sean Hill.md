**Sean Hill** is an improv performer, director, and teacher.  He is the founder of [[Theatres/The Hideout Theatre|The Hideout Theatre]], and ran the theater until 2009.

## History
A post from him about the first improv show he ever saw:
> First improv I ever saw was *[[Troupes/ComedySportz|ComedySportz]]* at their location on 6th St in Austin. Late 80's.
