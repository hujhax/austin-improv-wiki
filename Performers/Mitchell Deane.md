**Mitchell Deane** is an improv performer.

## History
Mitchell, born in the last century, is a native Austinite with a bold Austin accent. He studied Theatre since middle school, going on to receive a degree in Theatre (Acting/Directing), as well as a degree in Communication at Texas A&M Corpus Christi. He started improv while in college with a group called Islander Improv that later became Camp Danger. This drove him to pursue improv theatre related internships, which led him to [[Theatres/The Hideout Theatre|The Hideout Theatre]]. In the summer of 2010, he was at nearly every show, working in Box Office to Tech to House Management. Upon returning to school the next semester, he directed a fully improvised commedia dell'arte 50 minute play for his theatre capstone called *Commedia!*. After graduating, he returned to Austin where he got more involved in the improv scene, performing at [[Theatres/The Hideout|The Hideout]], [[Theatres/Salvage Vanguard|Salvage Vanguard]], [[Theatres/The Institution Theater|The Institution Theater]], and [[Theatres/Coldtowne Theater|Coldtowne Theater]] as well as completing all 6 levels of [[Theatres/The Hideout Theatre|The Hideout Theatre]]. In 2011, Mitchell went with [[Troupes/Parallelogramophonograph|Parallelogramophonograph]] to tech for them in the month long Edinburgh Fringe Festival in Edinburgh, Scotland. Later, Mitchell was given the honor of a [[Shows/Free Fringe|Free Fringe]] called Drowning in Mitchell Deane, in which various Austin improvisers did different short scenes or games, improvising as if they were Mitchell Deane. He was a member of [[Troupes/The Seven Eight Sevens|The Seven Eight Sevens]](a troupe centered around characters and places of Austin). These days, Mitchell performs in [[Shows/History Under the Influence|History Under the Influence]] (an improvised show based on Drunk History),and [[Shows/Flying Theatre Machine|Flying Theatre Machine]], a weekly improv show for kids.
