**Heidi Caldwell** is an improv performer.

## Summary
Heidi Caldwell has studied acting since her elementary school days which led to a career in TV, film and theater. She then forayed those talents into improv in 2006, by studying at some of the top improv schools in Los Angeles, including i.O. West and Second City.

She moved to Austin in 2009, and now performs regularly at [[Theatres/The Institution Theater|The Institution Theater]].   She has been working with children and young adults for many years, teaching improv workshops and entry-level improv classes.

Heidi is currently studying to be a Certified Therapeutic Recreational Specialist, with an emphasis on Creative Arts Therapy.
