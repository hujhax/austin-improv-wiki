**Ashley Nugent** is an actor, improviser, and improv teacher.

## History
Ashley has a theater and film background, but discovered improv while living in Arizona and completely fell in love. She moved to Los Angeles to study at iO West Theater, then moved to Austin to teach, coach, and perform. Ashley was on the Phoenix Improv Festival board for two years, traveled across the country with the musical improv troupe The Remainders. She performed with [[Troupes/Big Beautiful Warlock|Big Beautiful Warlock]] and currently performs as a member of [[Troupes/Nugefield|Nugefield]]. She was director of the [[Theatres/ColdTowne Conservatory|ColdTowne Conservatory]] Her most notable TV credit is of a victim on the SpikeTV show's First Season of 1000 Ways To Die.
