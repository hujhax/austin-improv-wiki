## History
Mary Henderson has her Bachelor of Fine Arts degree in Theatre from the University of Texas in Austin. She took her first improv class at the [[Hideout Theatre]] and was hooked for life. She continued by taking all levels at the [[Hideout Theatre]] including their first Summer Narrative Intensive in 2014. Along the way, she also picked up Levels 3 through 6 at [[Merlin Works]] along with their Singing Improv classes Levels 1 through 5. Mary completed levels 2 through 6 at [[Coldtowne Theater]] snd was chosen to be in their troupe [[Dear Bagel]] in 2017. She has performed in [[Fancy Pants]], [[Free Fringe]], [[Shows/Maestro]],and [[Shows/Maestro Raw]], the [[Molologue Jam]] (Winner May 2016) and [[Tightrope]].

Mary has participated in numerous improv workshops including Narration in Improv taught by [[Performers/Peter Rogers]] and [[Performers/Andreas Fabis]] at the Hideout Theatre, [[Performers/Sarah Marie Curry]]'s Viewpoints in Improv at the [[The Institution Theater]], [[Performers/Jessica Arjet]]'s Wonder Women workshops at the Hideout Theatre, and Jill Bernard's workshops on Solo Improv and Characterization.

She participated in [[Performers/Rob Yoho]]'s "Directing Improv" and [[Performers/Andy Crouch]]'s "Teaching Improv" classes.
