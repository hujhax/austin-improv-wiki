## History
Ellen began taking improv classes at the [[Theatres/Hideout Theatre|Hideout Theatre]] in November of 2010 as a way to make new friends in the new city of Austin. She continued through level six at the Hideout, and also participated in Levels 1 and 2 at [[Merlin Works]]. She performed in [[Shows/Maestro|Maestro]], [[Shows/Fancy Pants|Fancy Pants]], and a few [[Shows/Free Fringe|Free Fringe]] performances. In April of 2012, she was cast by [[Performers/Kevin Miller|Kevin Miller]] in the troupe [[Troupes/Kosher Bacon|Kosher Bacon]] at a MerlinWorks mixer. The troupe went on to perform a run of shows at the [[Theatres/Salvage Vanguard Theater|Salvage Vanguard Theater]] with their format "Underwaged". Later in 2012, Ellen participated as an adjunct performer and puppet tech for the black light puppet show "[[Shows/Off the Reef|Off the Reef]]" produced by [[Performers/Sara Farr|Sara Farr]] and directed by [[Performers/Dave Buckman|Dave Buckman]]. 

In December of 2012, she moved to the San Francisco Bay Area, and then married Tom Pizarek in June 2013. Ellen took levels 1 and 2 in San Francisco at Leela Improv. She also participated in two workshops facilitated by EndGames Improv. In February of 2015, Ellen and Tom moved back to Austin. Ellen has since performed in Maestro and FancyPants in the [[Festivals/46-Hour Improv Marathon|46-Hour Improv Marathon]]. She began Level 2 at the [[Theatres/Institution Theater|Institution Theater]] in August and took [[Performers/Jessica Arjet|Jessica Arjet]]'s four week Wonder Women workshop at the Hideout Theatre. She was cast in [[Shows/Tall Tales of the High Seas|Tall Tales of the High Seas]] which runs in February and March of 2016 at the [[Theatres/Institution Theater|Institution Theater]].
