**Nathan Sowell** is an improviser that can be found regularly playing at [[Theatres/Coldtowne Theater|Coldtowne Theater]]. He has gone through training at the Improv Olympic in Chicago and is a graduate of the ColdTowne Conservatory. Nathan started performing at an early age. His first production was a Stage Version of Young Frankenstein. He then went on to be a Youtube sensation for 3-4 months at the age of 15. He moved to Chicago for college. While visiting for Orientation, Nathan witnessed his very first Long Form Improv Show. It was the Armando Diaz Experience at iO Chicago. The speaker that night was Jason Chin, and he told honest, emotional stories that a bevy of talented performers then mined for comedic gold. That was when Nathan knew what he really wanted to do. Nathan started his improv career at the age of 19, and hasn't looked back since!
