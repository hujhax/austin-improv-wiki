**Steve Rogers** is a photographer who has worked frequently with the Austin improv community, both photographing shows and shooting publicity photos.

## Summary
After cutting his teeth shooting a couple of performances of *[[Dusk]]* and *[[Shows/The Sandbox]]*, he did primary photography for the first time on *[[Shows/Showdown]]* in 2011.

He has shot over 380 troupes and improv shows since then in all of the improv theaters.

Many troupes and festival use Steve's photos with his permission for their web sites and other related activities. Please feel free to contact him for more information.
