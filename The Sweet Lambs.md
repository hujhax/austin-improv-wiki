**The Sweet Lambs** are an improv duo.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2012 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>The Sweetlambs is made up of Austin improv luminaries Caitlin Sweet and Jeremy Lamb. They have over 25 years of combined experience in improv and less than one year of combined experience in being married. They attempt to create honest, well-rounded characters and gerthy narratives whether improvising in their daily lives or on stage for you.</blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2011 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>We're going to do something new most likely, either involving a short form game done over the course of a longform, a pre-set wheel of characters, or something musical.</blockquote>
