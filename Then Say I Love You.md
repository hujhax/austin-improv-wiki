**Then Say I Love You** (usually stylized as **...then say i love you**) was an improv troupe.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2011 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>...then say i love you presents improv comedy to warm your soul. we do long form improv that turns the garish and obscene into fun and whimsy. the ordinary becomes absurdly fanciful. our operatic arias will lull you into laughter. wherever you may roam...you'll always be at home... with ...then say i love you.</blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2011 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>we ALWAYS show up! we do long form...as long as you want. we just want to play and bring the fun!</blockquote>
