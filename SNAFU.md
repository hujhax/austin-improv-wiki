**SNAFU** is a UT long-form improv troupe.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2014 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>SNAFU is UT's only Long-Form Improv Troupe. Every week we bring stories to life in our trademark high-energy style.</blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2014 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>We alternate between The Living Room and Small Town La Ronde.</blockquote>
