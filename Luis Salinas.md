**Luis Salinas** is an improv performer who is associated primarily with [[The Institution Theatre]].

## History
Luis began his improv journey in the Fall of 2009, with the Improv 101 course at Merlin Works. After completing the 601 course in December of 2010, and mounting the show run for Bad Company, he immediately enrolled in a second round of 401-601 classes at Merlin Works (culminating the show run of The Trouble With Terry) as he simultaneously began his studies with the 301 course at the Institution Theater.
