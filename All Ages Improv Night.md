Every first Sunday of the month, ColdTowne Theater's youth program hosts two to three troupes comprised of performers under the age of 18. Youth shows are followed by a performance by Journey to the Big Water and culminate in a jam.

The goal of T.I.N. is to provide young improvisers with the opportunity to see both their peers and accomplished adult performers improvise full length shows. The jam which follows invites both audience members and performers to join Journey to the Big Water onstage for Austin's most unpredictable jam!
