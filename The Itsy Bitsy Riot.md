**The Itsy Bitsy Riot** was an improv troupe.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2011 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>The Itsy Bitsy Riot is a tornado of improvised comedy: you never know where it will land or how forceful it will be, but you can be sure anyone who sees it will get caught up in the madness and hilarity!</blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2011 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>We use an "open doors" format, wherein each scene is inspired by a line, detail, or theme from the scene which directly preceded it. Very high energy and lots of quick edits to keep the momentum going.</blockquote>
