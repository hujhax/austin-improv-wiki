Philip "Phil" Aulie was an improv performer, musician, and slam poet. Phil was a creator and founding member of [[The PITS]], [[Comedic Amoeba]], [[Parallelogramophonograph]], and [[You, Me, and Greg]]. The members of You, Me, and Greg (Phil Aulie, Greg Spencer, and Doug Pena) went on to form Superhouse[http://www.myspace.com/superhouserock], an indie pop rock band. Phil died unexpectedly of undisclosed causes at age 25 on January 30, 2012[http://www.legacy.com/obituaries/statesman/obituary.aspx?n=philip-david-aulie&pid=155772915].
