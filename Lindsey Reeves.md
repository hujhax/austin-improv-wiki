**Lindsey Reeves** is an improv performer.

## History
Lindsey started improv classes in spring of 2008 at [[The Hideout Theatre]], where [[Shana Merlin]] and [[Kareem Badr]] were her teachers.  Among her classmates were [[Andreas Fabis]], [[Brandon Salinas]], and [[Jen Kaplan]].

Lindsey will appear in [[The 44-Hour Improv Marathon]] as a core cast member.
