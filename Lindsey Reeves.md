**Lindsey Reeves** is an improv performer.

## History
Lindsey started improv classes in spring of 2008 at [[The Merlin Works Institute for Improvisation]], where [[Shana Merlin]] and [[Kareem Badr]] were her teachers.  Among her classmates were [[Andreas Fabis]], [[Brandon Salinas]], and [[Jen Kaplan]].

Lindsey appeared in [[The 44-Hour Improv Marathon]] as a core cast member.

A post from her about the first improv show she ever saw:<blockquote><p>My first experience watching an improv show was at the 2008 Short Fringe. [[Look Cookie]] performed after my group and I snuck into the audience to watch (and they actually talked about cookies), and then when we were in Best of Week, I listened to [[Girls Girls Girls]] while waiting backstage to go on. And I thought, oh, we're so not going to be in Best of Fest. And we weren't.</p> 

<p>I had taken 101 with [[Shana Merlin|Shana]] at the State in 2006 or 2007 and thought it was super-cool and fun. I hadn't imagined it as something one could make into a show, though, but a tool for being a better actor.</p> 

<p>Then last year I took 101 and 201 and saw [[Girls Girls Girls]] at the Hideout and then [[Get Up]] at [[FronteraFest]] this year and, well, now I want to eat it for breakfast.</p></blockquote>
