**Sunny Sweeney** was an improv performer.

## Summary
Sunny Sweeney performed with ComedySportz from 1997-1998, before moving to NYC for a year.  When she returned to Austin, she rejoined the CSz family as a waitress in the ComedySportz Playhouse.  

Sunny is an incredible singer.  In fact, she released her debut country music album, *Heartbreakers Hall of Fame* in 2006.  In 2009, she was the first artist signed to the newly formed Republic Nashville.  Her follow-up album *From a Table Away* (2010) featured the hit single "From a Table Away" which climbed to #10 on the country charts in 2010. 

Sunny has played the Grand Ole Opry multiple times and is constantly on the road with her unique style of twangy Texas music.

In 2013, she signed a new record deal with Thirty Tigers Records, and is currently working on her third album.  

She is married to a police officer and currently lives in Austin.
