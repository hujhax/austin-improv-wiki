**The 41-Hour Improv Marathon** was the second annual [[Festivals/Hideout Improv Marathon|Hideout Improv Marathon]].  It was performed from 6/4/2010 to 6/6/2010.

## Core Players
* [[Performers/Bill Stern|Bill Stern]] 
* [[Performers/Curtis Luciani|Curtis Luciani]]
* [[Performers/Jessica Arjet|Jessica Arjet]]
* [[Performers/Kaci Beeler|Kaci Beeler]]
* [[Performers/Marc Majcher|Marc Majcher]]
* [[Performers/Meghan Wolff|Meghan Wolff]]
* [[Performers/Roy Janik|Roy Janik]]
* [[Performers/Valerie Ward|Valerie Ward]]

## Schedule
* Friday 4pm: "Flowey Donut", free-form improv with the core improvisors
* Friday 5pm: *[[Shows/Maestro|Maestro]]*
* Friday 7pm: [[Troupes/Junk|Junk]] presents "Live... and Let Die!"
* Friday 8pm: "Electric Bill", musical improv with [[Performers/Bill Stern|Bill Stern]]
* Friday 9pm: [[Troupes/Confidence Men|Confidence Men]]
* Friday 10pm: *[[Shows/The Nightmare Video Project|The Nightmare Video Project]]* with [[Troupes/Parallelogramophonograph|Parallelogramophonograph]] 
* Friday 11pm: [[Troupes/Girls Girls Girls|Girls Girls Girls]]
* Saturday 12am: [[Shows/Dubbed Indemnity|Dubbed Indemnity]]
* Saturday 1am: [[Troupes/Sarah 7|Sarah 7]] presents improv based on true stories
* Saturday 2am: [[Troupes/The Love Notes|The Love Notes]] present                          improvised Carmen San Diego
* Saturday 3am: [[Troupes/Snackers|Snackers]] present "Late Night Staff Meeting"
* Saturday 4am: "Buddies!", an improvised buddy sitcom.
* Saturday 5am: [[Troupes/Anticdotes|Anticdotes]]
* Saturday 6am: [[Troupes/Firth & Arjet|Firth & Arjet]] present "Always a Bridesmaid"
* Saturday 7am: [[Troupes/Rise & Shine|Rise & Shine]]
* Saturday 8am: musical montage-improv with [[Troupes/Pretty Jack Jaybird|Pretty Jack Jaybird]]
* Saturday 9am: [[Troupes/Editor and Chief|Editor and Chief]] presents improvised Dungeons & Dragons
* Saturday 10am: [[Troupes/The Dancy Street D'Orchestra|The Dancy Street D'Orchestra]]
* Saturday 11am: [[Troupes/ComedySportz|ComedySportz]]
* Saturday 12pm: Student Show
* Saturday 1pm: [[Troupes/Your Dad's Friends|Your Dad's Friends]] presents "Cliff's Notes"
* Saturday 2pm: [[Shows/Flying Theater Machine|Flying Theater Machine]]
* Saturday 3pm: Student Show
* Saturday 4pm: [[Troupes/Improv For Evil|Improv For Evil]]
* Saturday 5pm: *[[Shows/Dusk|Dusk]]*
* Saturday 6pm: *[[Shows/Austin Secrets|Austin Secrets]]*
* Saturday 7pm: [[Troupes/The Knuckleball Now|The Knuckleball Now]]
* Saturday 8pm: *[[Shows/The Andersons|The Andersons]]*
* Saturday 9pm: Improv based on *1001 Monologues for Teens*
* Saturday 10pm: *[[Shows/Maestro|Maestro]]*
* Sunday 12am: [[Puppet Improv Project]]
* Sunday 1am: [[Troupes/Your Terrific Neighbors|Your Terrific Neighbors]] present "Parlour Games"
* Sunday 2am: [[Troupes/Look Cookie|Look Cookie]] presents "Sleepover"
* Sunday 3am: ["The Bat"](http://improvencyclopedia.org/games/The_Bat.html) with [[Troupes/ColdTowne (Troupe)|ColdTowne]]
* Sunday 4am: [[Troupes/Midnight Society|Midnight Society]] presents "Late Night Talk Show"
* Sunday 5am: [[Troupes/The Starter Kit|The Starter Kit]]
	* They presented "Professor Dobson's Time Travel Adventure", which was in some ways a progenitor of [[Troupes/The Professor|The Professor]].
* Sunday 6am: [[Shows/Holy 1960s Batman, Batman!|"Holy Sleep Deprivation, Batman!"]], an improvised episode of the 60s *Batman* tv show
* Sunday 7am: [[Troupes/The Frank Mills|The Frank Mills]]
* Sunday 8am: "The Final Countdown", free-form improv with the core improvisors
