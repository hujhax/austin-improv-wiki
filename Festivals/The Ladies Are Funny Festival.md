***The Ladies Are Funny Festival*** (AKA **LAFF**) is an annual comedy festival that showcases female talent.

## History
The festival was founded by [[Troupes/Girls Girls Girls|Girls Girls Girls]] in 2007.

NYC sketch troupe Skinny Bitch Jesus Meeting have performed in every LAFF since its inception.

In 2012, the festival introduced short films into their program.

The festival went on hiatus in 2013, but returned in 2014.
