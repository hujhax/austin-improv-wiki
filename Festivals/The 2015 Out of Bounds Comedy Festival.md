**The 2014 Out of Bounds Comedy Festival** was the fourteenth annual [[Festivals/Out of Bounds Comedy Festival|Out of Bounds Comedy Festival]].

## Summary
Its advertising used basic logos for improv (lightning in a bottle), podcasting (a desktop mic), sketch (a pencil), and stand-up (a stage mic).

The festival ran from from Tuesday 9/1/13 to Monday 9/7/13.

This discontinued the festival tradition of having an annual roast, but did include the traditional all-star *[[Shows/Maestro|Maestro]]*.
