**The 2012 Out of Bounds Comedy Festival** was the eleventh annual [[Festivals/Out of Bounds Comedy Festival|Out of Bounds Comedy Festival]].

## Summary
Its advertising was themed around the end of the world, and referred to the festival as "OOBageddon".

It featured Scott Adsit, John Lutz, and L. A. improv troupe Beer Shark Mice as headliners.

The festival ran from Tuesday 8/28/12 to Monday 9/3/11.

It included a performance of [[Shows/Blue Maestro|Blue Maestro]], as well as "ZEDTalks", a set of TEDTalks-like presentations on random subjects set to randomly-selected PowerPoint presentations.

The festival roast featured [[Performers/David Lampe|David Lampe]].
