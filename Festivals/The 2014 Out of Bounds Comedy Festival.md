**The 2014 Out of Bounds Comedy Festival** was the thirteenth annual [[Festivals/Out of Bounds Comedy Festival|Out of Bounds Comedy Festival]].

## Summary
Its advertising was chemistry-themed.

The festival ran from from Tuesday 8/26/13 to Monday 9/1/13.

The festival roast featured [[Performers/Andy Crouch|Andy Crouch]].
