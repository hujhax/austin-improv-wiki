**WaffleFest 2013** was the twelfth annual [[Festivals/Wafflefest|Wafflefest]].

## Summary
The festival ran from 11/21/13 to 11/23/13.

It included an all-star *[[Shows/Maestro|Maestro]]* and a teen showcase.
