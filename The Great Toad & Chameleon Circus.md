**The Great Toad & Chameleon Circus** was a one-off *[[Cagematch]]* duo.

## History
They showed up to play a Cagematch against [[Firth & Arjet]] on 5/25/07, but no audience members showed up.

They then teamed up with [[Firth & Arjet]] to take on [[A Bride Soon]] on 6/9/07.  (They lost.)
