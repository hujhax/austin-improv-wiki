Wait OK Now was formed in January 2012 as part of GNAP's student troupes, cast out of Merlin Works mixers. It originally consisted of Sam Schak, Jen Kaplan, Lacy Shawn, Luke Wallens, Tai Hovanky, Nick Jimenez, and Caeriel Crestin, with Ruby Willman as coach/director. The troupe did a run of shows at SVT performing an improvised sitcom called The Good Life. They are currently on indefinite hiatus.
