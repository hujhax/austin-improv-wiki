**Worst First Date** is an improv duo.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2015 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>Worst First Date is a duo that explores the thrill, the joy, the terror, and the complete awkwardness of the first date. In every show two new characters meet for the very first time...and things go downhill from there.</blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2015 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>Each show we do is a first date, told in a fairly linear format (with the occasional single-character flashback to fill in information). We play the same characters from beginning to end. Our performances are all about the ways those two characters interact with each other and the dynamic that develops between them. To keep the promise in our troupe name, the date will deteriorate and become terrible. We're always gonna shoot for The Worst. The deterioration of the date may be due to internal (character conflict) or external (narrative event) factors that will be discovered as the show is played. The escalating terribleness of the first date may result in the two characters hating each other, falling madly in love, or anything in between.</blockquote>
