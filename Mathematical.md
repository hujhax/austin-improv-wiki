**Mathematical** (often written with an exclamation point: **Mathematical!**) was an improv troupe.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2013 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>Mathematical! is a two man improvised theater troupe based in Austin, Texas featuring Jason Oliver and Zach Moren. We create multiple characters and stories to tell a unique story.</blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2013 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>We do an abbreviated version of a two man harold based on an opening conversation between the two of us inspired by a word from our audience.</blockquote>
