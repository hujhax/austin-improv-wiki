**Guilds of Steel** was a narrative longform show based on [[Wikipedia - Massively multiplayer online role-playing game|MMORPG]]s such as *[[Wikipedia - World of Warcraft|World of Warcraft]]*.

## History
There were two runs of *Guilds of Steel*.

The first was in May and June of 2009, and was directed by [[Bryan Roberts]] and [[Michael Joplin]].

The second was called *Guilds of Steel: The Reign of Haggoth*, ran in July 2010, and was directed solely by [[Bryan Roberts]].
