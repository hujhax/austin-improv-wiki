**G.O.O.F.S.** is an improv troupe.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2014 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>5 genre suggestions. 5 improvised pitches. You choose the film you want to see, and G.O.O.F.S. bring it to life.</blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2013 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>We do cinematic narrative. At the start of the show we'll get genres and suggestions from the audience for each of the 5 of us. One by one we'll give a 30 second pitch for a film based on the suggestions and the audience will vote on the movie they'd like to see brought to life. This takes about 3-4 minutes. The rest of our set will be the improvised "acting out" of the story.</blockquote>
