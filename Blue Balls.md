**Blue Balls** was a one-off *[[Cagematch]]* team.

## Summary
The show put a set of six "hindrances" in a hat: donuts, alcohol, running shoes, cough syrup, and a couple of pharmacological options.  Each performer then picked out two of the hindrances, ingested them (or smoked them, or, in the case of the running shoes, ran in them), and, thus hindered, started the show.

Rachel wound up drinking a glass of vodka followed by a shot of cough syrup.  Dave had to run to the Capitol building and back.  Andy ate a half-dozen donuts.  (By the end of the show, he sat center stage and was unable to move which made things fun and interesting during scene edits.)

A framed picture of the performance is on the wall of the upstairs Hideout bathroom.

## History
The troupe played a Cagematch on 2/3/06.
