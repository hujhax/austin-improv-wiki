**1813 Drake** was an improv duo (with one cast change).

## Summary
In 2006 roommates [[Andy Crouch]] and [[Jay Bernardo]] decided to start performing as a duo under the name 1813 Drake, their then address in South Austin. Their usual format was a two-man [[Living Room]] in which they checked in with each other, discussing honestly whatever came to mind and then playing scenes inspired by the conversations. When Jay moved to Taiwan in 2007, [[Asaf Ronen]] was moving to Austin from New York City and ended up joining Andy at 1813 Drake as his new roommate and *in* 1813 Drake as his new troupe mate. 1813 Drake ceased to be when Andy moved in with his girlfriend. Asaf kept the apartment and was joined by [[Tom Booker]] (with whom he already had a duo called [[Baxter and Bernard]].

## History
They performed regularly in shows at [[The Hideout Theatre]] and occasionally [[ColdTowne]].
