**Ed32** was a short-form improv troupe founded by [[Performers/Andy Crouch|Andy Crouch]] and based out of UT. It was the first improv troupe for several active improvisers, including [[Performers/Michael Ferstenfeld|Michael Ferstenfeld]], [[Performers/Curtis Luciani|Curtis Luciani]], [[Performers/Caitlin Sweetlamb|Caitlin Sweetlamb]], and [[Performers/Valerie Ward|Valerie Ward]].

The troupe was named for founding member Ed Piston, who was 32 at the time of the troupe's founding, making him at least a decade older than the other members. The name persisted after Ed left the troupe, and a troupe t-shirt included a picture of him.
