**Ricochet** is an improv troupe founded by Kris Pina, and composed of performers mostly new to formal improv performance.

## Summary
Ricochet studies and performs short-form improv, with an emphasis on the so-called "Armando" format using short monologues to introduce their 20-25 minute sets.

## History
* 1/31/18 - Hyde Park Theatre *Frontera Fest* ("Girl Scout Cookies")
