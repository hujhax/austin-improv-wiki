**Bitches Be Crazy** is an improv-duo offshoot of [[Troupes/IScream Sandwich|IScream Sandwich]].

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2015 application to perform at [[Theatres/The Hideout Theatre|The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>Carissa and Heidi are two founding members of the well known Austin Harold Team - iScream Sandwich. At a recent improv festival in Dallas, the two ladies were the only ones to make it and together the two besties discovered the awesome story telling variation of the Harold format they call a Living Room Harold. Based on a life experience suggested by an audience member - the dazzling duo tap into their vast life experiences to inspire scenes and group games! </blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2015 application to perform at [[Theatres/The Hideout Theatre|The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>We do a living room style, story telling opening and then do a format loosely based on the Harold. </blockquote>
