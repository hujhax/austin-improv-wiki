**The Darkness** was an improv troupe that performed the Bat.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2013 application to perform at [[Theatres/The Hideout Theatre|The Hideout Theatre]]:
> 
> The Darkness is a collaboration between students and alumni from [[Theatres/The New Movement|The New Movement]] and Hideout Theatres, which began after each theatre had a bat workshop held by Justin Strackany from The New Movement. 
> 
> And what is the bat? 
> 
> The bat is improv in the dark. 
> 
> No lights. 
> 
> Just several people seated on stage telling a story through primal sounds, phrases, singular words and emotional conduct.
> 


### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2013 application to perform at [[Theatres/The Hideout Theatre|The Hideout Theatre]]:
> 
> We do the bat. 
> 
> Our performance is in the dark. 
> 
> Sometimes with ambient sound. 
> 
> Sometimes with nothing but our voices and human harmonics to create a story, a "narrative," organically through phrases, single words and primal sounds. 
> 
> We're an offspring of a Joe Bill workshop that prompted two similar workshops at both the Hideout and New Movement. 
> 
> Students from both workshops have shown a strong interest in doing more bat improv in and around Austin
> so we're looking at theatres to play at.
>
