*Rise & Shine* was an improvised morning talk show parody of *Regis & Kelly* and regional/local morning talk shows.  Each episode took place in real time as if it were being recorded (a monoscene).

The cast played the same characters every time & invited a guest player to improvise a fictional celebrity. 

## Cast & Roles
* [[Performers/Valerie Ward|Valerie Ward]] as Lorelei Reiser, one of the hosts.
* [[Performers/Curtis Luciani|Curtis Luciani]] as Elmo Shein, one of the hosts.
* [[Performers/Jon Bolden|Jon Bolden]] as J. Robert Sturrier, their producer.

## History
*Rise & Shine* started in January, 2009 and stopped performing sometime in late 2010.

The first performance was a [[Shows/Cagematch|Cagematch]] show and happened on Saturday, March 7th, 2009 at [[Theatres/Coldtowne Theater|Coldtowne Theater]]. It featured [[Performers/John Ratliff|John Ratliff]] as the guest celebrity.
