**Super Sweet** is an improv troupe.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2015 application to perform at [[Theatres/The Hideout Theatre|The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>Hey yo were super sweet and we're coming back at you. 

We bonded growing up hard on the mean streets of the hideout theater levels 1-7 and are ready to open a can of improv whoop ass on stage.</blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2015 application to perform at [[Theatres/The Hideout Theatre|The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>We work mostly in montage format with a very loose structure to give us room to be as playful with the audience as well as with each other. </blockquote>
