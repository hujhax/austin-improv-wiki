**New Moustache** was an improv troupe.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2011 application to perform at [[Theatres/The Hideout Theatre|The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote><p>New Moustache consists of an ad man, a documentarian, a radio personality and a retired jailer.</p> 

<p>They are breaking out of the Institution (Theater) and into the hearts of improvised comedy fans. Join New Moustache on a whirlwind montage of improvised scenes interspersed with dynamic character monologues and a touch of heightened reality.</p></blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2010 application to perform at [[Theatres/The Hideout Theatre|The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote><p>We take a one-word suggestion from the audience and use that to begin a montage of scenes.</p> 
<p>We also focus on one-person scenes and character monologues.</p> 
<p>Some of our one-person scenes have multiple characters that another performer may step into.</p></blockquote>
