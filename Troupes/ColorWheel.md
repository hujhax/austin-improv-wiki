**ColorWheel** (also written **Colorwheel**) was an improv troupe.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2010 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote><p>ColorWheel is a lusty trio made of:</p> 

<p>Jay Byrd, John Buseman, and Christine Giordano.</p> 

<p>They harmonize and match energies that drive high-power scenes.</p> 

<p>Weird and wonderful characters emerge to take audiences on a journey through their world with a high sense of play.</p> </blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2010 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>We utilize matching energies for scene starts and organic transformations for our edits.</blockquote>
