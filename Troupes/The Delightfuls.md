**The Delightfuls** are an improv troupe.

## History
The troupe was formed around the time of [[Festivals/The 2013 Out of Bounds Comedy Festival|The 2013 Out of Bounds Comedy Festival]] when the three girls started hanging out together as a trio, and had a craft date wherein they created "dream boards".

## Summary
Their style consists of positive, relatively quick-paced scenes based on true stories of their lives.
