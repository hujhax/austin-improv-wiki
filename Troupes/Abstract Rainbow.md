**Abstract Rainbow** was an improv troupe.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2010 application to perform at [[Theatres/The Hideout Theatre|The Hideout Theatre]]:
> Abstract Rainbow is a puppet making company based in Austin Texas that also performs live comedy variety shows featuring original Abstract Rainbow puppets. The live show is a variety act involving sketch, improv, singing, dancing and puppetry.


### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2010 application to perform at [[Theatres/The Hideout Theatre|The Hideout Theatre]]:
> We're a comedy variety act that performs improv and sketch using song, dance and puppetry. <br>We have an opening song and dance number, then we alternate between improvised scenes, scripted sketches with puppets, improvised songs and dances and scripted songs and dances.
