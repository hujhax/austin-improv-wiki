**Hank and Marlena** was a musical duo that originated as part of [[Troupes/There's Waldo|There's Waldo]].

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2012 application to perform at [[Theatres/The Hideout Theatre|The Hideout Theatre]]:
> Country music's most beloved and in love duo, Marlena Mae Mercer & Hanklin T. Belle will delight your minds and steal your hearts. Please welcome 12-time Country Music Award attendees and creators of Country Fried: A Home-Cooked Variety Show!


### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2012 application to perform at [[Theatres/The Hideout Theatre|The Hideout Theatre]]:
> We perform original music as an exaggerated tribute to Loretta Lynn and Conway Twitty.
