**Silent Letters** (formerly **Men in Chrissy's Life**) is an improv troupe.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2013 application to perform at [[Theatres/The Hideout Theatre|The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>A boss. An ex-boyfriend. A co-worker. An old college bro. And a girl. 

Men in Chrissy's Life brings together a show that tells the stories of difficult relationships and characters leading up to a very, very special event. Truth and hijinx improvised</blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2013 application to perform at [[Theatres/The Hideout Theatre|The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote><p>We ask for a suggestion of a special event and then our show tells the story of people awaiting and leading up to that special event in their lives.</p> 

<p>We stay in one world and usually play only 1 to 2 characters each.</p> 

<p>We begin grounded and ramp the pace and lean into the crazy near the middle and then ground out our world at the end.</p> </blockquote>
