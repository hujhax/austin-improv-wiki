**Bad Font** is an independent, Harold oriented, improv team.

## History
Bad Font was originally cast by [[Performers/Scott Hearne|Scott Hearne]] throughout 2012 and 2013. The team is primarily coached by [[Performers/Dave Buckman|Dave Buckman]]. Former coaches are [[Performers/Clifton Highfield|Clifton Highfield]], [[Performers/Chris McKeever|Chris McKeever]], and [[Performers/Bill Stern|Bill Stern]]. In 2014, [[Performers/Carlos Larotta|Carlos Larotta]] and [[Performers/Kristen Kurtis|Kristen Kurtis]] joined the team. In 2015, [[Performers/Cene Hale|Cene Hale]] and [[Performers/Alex Ayala|Alex Ayala]] were added to the team. In Spring 2016, Megan Mowry was added. From September 2015 to July 2016, [[Performers/Scott Hearne|Scott Hearne]] was on hiatus. In July 2016, Bad Font added: [[Performers/Kelly Campbell|Kelly Campbell]], Rachel Denton, [[Performers/Michael Joplin|Michael Joplin]], Griffin May, and Johnny Sullivan. 

[[Performers/Justin Davidson|Justin Davidson]] originated the name Bad Font. The team chose the name because it is easy to spell and pronounce, it contains multiple meanings, and is mildly amusing. Oh! And no one hated the name.
