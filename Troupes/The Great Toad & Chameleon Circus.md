**The Great Toad & Chameleon Circus** was a one-off *[[Shows/Cagematch|Cagematch]]* duo.

## History
They showed up to play a Cagematch against [[Troupes/Firth & Arjet|Firth & Arjet]] on 5/25/07, but no audience members showed up.

They then teamed up with [[Troupes/Firth & Arjet|Firth & Arjet]] to take on [[Troupes/A Bride Soon|A Bride Soon]] on 6/9/07.  (They lost.)
