**PJ Party** is an improv duo.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2013 application to perform at [[Theatres/The Hideout Theatre|The Hideout Theatre]]:
> Paul and Judith met under ordinary circumstances -- a class in college. But their friendship transpired into something more than extraordinary. This truly unique duo makes audiences laugh by the power crazy characters and surprising dialogue.


### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2013 application to perform at [[Theatres/The Hideout Theatre|The Hideout Theatre]]:
> We are a duo that focus on slow scenework. We are very patient with each other and utilize our time on stage to create complex characters and strong relationships. Sometimes we try to do a modified version of the deconstruction, but we mainly seek to let ourselves and the characters we develop to go with the flow of the time we have on stage.
