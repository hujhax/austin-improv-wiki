## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2013 application to perform at [[Theatres/The Hideout Theatre|The Hideout Theatre]]:
> Like recounting awkward teenage memories or complaining about your soul-sucking office job, Royston crafts comedy out of schadenfreude. Sure, we kick you while your down, but we always help you get back up. 


### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2013 application to perform at [[Theatres/The Hideout Theatre|The Hideout Theatre]]:
> We do a "Groundhog Day" monoscene format.
