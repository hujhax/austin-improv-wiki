**Sis BOOM Bah!** is an Austin improv troupe active since around 2015. Primary members are [[Jason Burnett]], [[Johnathan Simpson]], [[Performers/Stuart Feild|Stuart Feild]]. They perform a modified living room herald with an emphasis on rapid cuts and heighten comedy over narrative form and structure, a style they refer to as "Three Layer Burrito".
