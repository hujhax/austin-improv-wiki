**The Photon's Mistress** was an improv duo.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2011 application to perform at [[Theatres/The Hideout Theatre|The Hideout Theatre]]:
> The Photon's Mistress is Jessica and Luis Salinas, who have been married for over 25 years.
>  
> 
> They don't just finish each others' sentences, they speak each others thoughts.
>  
> 
> As The Photon's Mistress, they weave a retro-futurist narrative of how we imagined the world to come.


### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2011 application to perform at [[Theatres/The Hideout Theatre|The Hideout Theatre]]:
> We aim to tell science fiction stories grounded in the past.
>  
> 
> What did people fear in the '40s? What technologies did they imagine the future would bring?
