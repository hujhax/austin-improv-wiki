**The Photon's Mistress** was an improv duo.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2011 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote><p>The Photon's Mistress is Jessica and Luis Salinas, who have been married for over 25 years.</p> 

<p>They don't just finish each others' sentences, they speak each others thoughts.</p> 

<p>As The Photon's Mistress, they weave a retro-futurist narrative of how we imagined the world to come.</p></blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2011 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote><p>We aim to tell science fiction stories grounded in the past.</p> 

<p>What did people fear in the '40s? What technologies did they imagine the future would bring?</p></blockquote>
