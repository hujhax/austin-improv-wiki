**The Ones** is an improv troupe.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2014 application to perform at [[Theatres/The Hideout Theatre|The Hideout Theatre]]:
> The Ones kick it out with naive elements while telling a story.
>  
> 
> Some of us won't know what's going on.
>  
> 
> But you as the audience will always know our secrets.


### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2014 application to perform at [[Theatres/The Hideout Theatre|The Hideout Theatre]]:
> We do a three scene narrative but some of the players do not see the first scene.
>  
> 
> We tell a story with naive aspects.
