**Comedic Amoeba** was a high-school improv troupe.

## Summary
The troupe was formed by high school students out of Westwood High School in North Austin. They formed in 2005 out of the remnants of [[Troupes/The PITS|The PITS]]. They performed two shows at [[Theatres/The Hideout Theatre|The Hideout Theatre]] in the summer of 2005. They performed both shortform games and longform narrative improv. One of their performances was a longform narrative murder mystery format they originated.
