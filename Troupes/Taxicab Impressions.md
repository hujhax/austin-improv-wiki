**Taxi Cab Impressions** is a predominantly short-form Armando-style troupe with a rotating cast pool.

## Summary
The director and resident cab driver, [[Performers/Brent Unger|Brent Unger]], shares his experiences as a taxi driver and audience members share their own experiences in taxis and those stories are used as inspiration for improvised scenes.  The troupe was formed in February 2013 after a very successful first show at [[Theatres/The Hideout Theatre|The Hideout Theatre]]'s *[[Shows/Free Fringe|Free Fringe]]*.

Current cast members are [[Performers/Ryan Hill|Ryan Hill]], [[Performers/Heidi Rogers|Heidi Rogers]], [[Performers/Paul Normandin|Paul Normandin]], [[Performers/Andreas Fabis|Andreas Fabis]], [[Performers/Luke Wallens|Luke Wallens]], [[Performers/Dana Yanoshak|Dana Yanoshak]], [[Performers/Kathy Rose Center|Kathy Rose Center]], [[Performers/Chuy Zarate|Chuy Zarate]], [[Performers/Jessica Arjet|Jessica Arjet]], [[Performers/Brad Hawkins|Brad Hawkins]], [[Performers/Chris Allen|Chris Allen]] and [[Performers/Mike Nystul|Mike Nystul]].
