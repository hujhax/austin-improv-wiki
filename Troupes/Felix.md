**Felix** is an improv duo.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2014 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>John Ratliff and Juliet Prather are a study in contrasts: young and old, short and tall, woman and man, mind-blowingly beautiful and mind-blowingly handsome. And yet they meet as kindred spirits on the ancient plain of improv, where none of this matters, except that it's funnier when she plays a big ol' biker dude or he acts like a fancy lady. </blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2014 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>Grounded scenework.</blockquote>
