**Minority Report** is an improv troupe.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2015 application to perform at [[Theatres/The Hideout Theatre|The Hideout Theatre]]:
> Everyone has flaws, and instead of hiding them, we'll be flaunting them! Join us as weave a narrative based on the audience's funniest flaws.


### "What's Your Deal?"
Minority Report highlights the vastly different personalities, backgrounds, and ethnicities of five women in the 20s, 30s, and 40s. They perform a format they invented called the “Living MonoPOP”, in which the women play themselves in a naturalist monoscene, telling true stories and offering unique perspectives. Peppered throughout the monoscene are fun and absurd pop outs!
