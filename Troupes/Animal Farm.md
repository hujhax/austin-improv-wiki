**Animal Farm** was an improv troupe.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2012 application to perform at [[Theatres/The Hideout Theatre|The Hideout Theatre]]:
> Three of Austin's most physical players bring you one of Austin's most delightful shows - Animal Farm.
>  
> 
> Jessica Arjet, Sarah Marie Curry, and Asaf Ronen take your animal suggestion and evolve on stage before your very eyes.
>  
> 
> Their amazing physicality and larger than life characters played with total sincerity lead to playful hilarity.
>  
> 
> All improv is equal, but some improv is more equal than others.
>  


### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2011 application to perform at [[Theatres/The Hideout Theatre|The Hideout Theatre]]:
> We get suggestions of Animals from the audience and use those to create characters who then create a story.
>  
> 
> We came up with the night that we thought we were performing, but then ended up not.
>  
> 
> It was really fun at the time, and Sarah Marie and I play together in 3 different troupes/shows right now, so we are comfortable together.
>  
> 
> Very physical, but also dramatic, we are fun stuff.
