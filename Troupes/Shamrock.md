**Shamrock** is an improv duo featuring [[Performers/Ryan Hill|Ryan Hill]] and [[Performers/Scott Hearne|Scott Hearne]].

## Format
Shamrock is a game focused comedic montage that opens with a three minute pattern creation game known as a cloverleaf.

## History
Scott and Ryan were the only two members of [[Troupes/The Seven Eight Sevens|The Seven Eight Sevens]] for the entirety of its life. When that project ended, they decided to start a duo.
