**Ames and James** was an improv troupe.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2011 application to perform at [[Theatres/The Hideout Theatre|The Hideout Theatre]]:
> Amy McKenna is a long-time Terrytown resident with two dogs and a penchant for macrame owls. 
> 
> James Roberts is a computer programmer who dreams of driving a big rig across America at least once before he dies. 
> 
> Together, they are Ames and James -- delivering comedy and engaging characters while James struggles to keep up with Amy.


### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2011 application to perform at [[Theatres/The Hideout Theatre|The Hideout Theatre]]:
> We've been doing a lot of history-based scene work where we jump around time and play characters that might be found at famous battles or civil rights marches or doing cave paintings in pre-historic times.
