**Lucky Billy** is an improv troupe in Austin, TX that creates and performs Chicago-style longform improv forms.  

## History
Lucky Billy was one of two Beta improv teams formed at the former New Movement (Austin) theater, cast by Nathan Ehrman. Following the metamorphosis of TNM ATX into Fallout, the two teams merged into one.
