**Gravy** was an improv troupe.

## History
The troupe performed a reunion show at [[Theatres/The Hideout Theatre|The Hideout Theatre]] in 2014.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2014 application to perform at [[Theatres/The Hideout Theatre|The Hideout Theatre]]:
> Gravy was a powerhouse supergroup from the mid 2000s focusing on organic flow within the constrained "Elevator" format. There are a fun, experienced pack of jackasses who follow their every instinct on stage without too much forethought to the consequences.


### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2014 application to perform at [[Theatres/The Hideout Theatre|The Hideout Theatre]]:
> Utilizing frequent pop-outs in the "Elevator" format, the group weaves broken narratives from the past and future while tying together the present situation the characters are met with.


## History
The troupe performed their last show at [[Festivals/The 2004 Out of Bounds Comedy Festival|The 2004 Out of Bounds Comedy Festival]].
