**Fort Knox** was a one-off *[[Shows/Cagematch|Cagematch]]* team.

## Summary
The show featured improvised songs with the improvisers playing musical instruments on stage.

It was a monoscene of musicians rehearsing together to create a song.

The scene ended when the song was over.

## History
They played one Cagematch on 4/10/08.
