**Kopp & Buddy: Buddy Cops** was an improv troupe.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2014 application to perform at [[Theatres/The Hideout Theatre|The Hideout Theatre]]:
> He's a streetwise New Yorker. He's a redneck from down South. Together, they are Kopp & Buddy: Buddy Cops. Angry police captains. Oddly German villains. Nagging ex-wives. Resentful teenage kids. Alimony payments, drinking problems, and chest pains----Kopp and Buddy have it all.


### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2014 application to perform at [[Theatres/The Hideout Theatre|The Hideout Theatre]]:
> While we are doing a "buddy cop film" format, we're focused less on a plot and a lot more on these two characters (played by Andrew and Nathan). Our goal is to strike a gamey, playful rapport in the midst of a bunch of "buddy cop" cliches. We want to dig in, not out.
