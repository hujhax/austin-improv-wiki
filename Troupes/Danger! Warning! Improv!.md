**Danger! Warning! Improv!** is an improv duo that focuses on fast-paced, high-production-value narrative genre improv with heavy audience involvement.

## Summary
In a Danger! Warning! Improv!, both actors play all the parts, trading parts back and forth frequently.  Each format has appropriate costumes -- e.g., *Star Trek* uniforms for "Two to Beam Up" or tuxedos for "Secret Agent Men" -- and the duo uses an onstage sound board for soundtrack and SFX cues.  They often bring up audience volunteers, for example to serve as Bond girls for "Secret Agent Men".

## Formats
The troupe primarily works in specific genres for each show.

So far, their formats have been "Secret Agent Men" (James Bond), "Two to Beam Up" (*classic Star Trek* -- also known as "Double Tribble"), and "Elementary, Dear Holmes" (classic Sherlock Holmes).
