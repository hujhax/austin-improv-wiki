**Sugarbakers** was an improv troupe.

## History
They played in *[[Shows/The Cagematch]]* on 2/6/13, against [[Troupes/Hyena]].

The troupe went on hiatus in 2013, when member [[Performers/Kayla Lane Freeman]] moved to Chicago.
