**Sarah 7** was an all-female improv troupe.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
This is their bio from the [[Festivals/2012 Out of Bounds|2012 Out of Bounds]] web site:

> "Sarah 7 takes an input from the audience, and tells a series of short personal monologues inspired by the suggestion. Mining those monologues for details and meaning, the seven ladies perform character-driven improv scenes that wind together the experiences derived from the source word.
> 
> 
> Sarah 7 has performed their monologue-inspired, character-driven improv at [[Festivals/The Ladies Are Funny Festival|The Ladies Are Funny Festival]], [[Festivals/The 42-Hour Improv Marathon|The 42-Hour Improv Marathon]], 2010 and 2011 [[Festivals/Wafflefest|Wafflefest]]s, 2010 Frontera Fest, and the [[Festivals/The 2010 Out of Bounds Comedy Festival|2010]] and [[Festivals/The 2011 Out of Bounds Comedy Festival|The 2011 Out of Bounds Comedy Festival]]. They have hosted several themed show runs, including 'Life Lessons Your Mother Never Taught You', 'Sarah 7: Your Worst Nightmare', 'Holidays are Terrible with Sarah 7' and 'Douchebags and Pantyliners: Commercial Deconstruction'."


## History
Sarah 7 headlined *[[Shows/The Threefer|The Threefer]]* in October 2012.
