**Duplex** is an improv troupe made up of recent graduates of [[The Hideout Theatre]].

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2014 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>Duplex is an improv troupe consisting of four people, two front doors, and a shared backyard. Both reasonably priced and in a good neighborhood, Duplex is fun, smart, fast-paced improv for anyone who wished their neighbors lived closer, or their roommates lived farther away.</blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2014 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote><p>We're 4 Hideout alumni who do a traditional montage show.</p>

<p>We have been experimenting with formats, but have not decided on doing a specific one beyond traditional montage.</p></blockquote>
