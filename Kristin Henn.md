Kristin Henn is a performer in Austin, TX. She graduated from the ColdTowne Conservatory in May of 2010.

Kristin performs with Oh, Science and Big Beautiful Warlock. She directs and performs with What's the Story Steve.

Kristin oversees and facilitates the youth program at ColdTowne Theater via her performing arts organization, Move Your Tale. Additionally, she organizes the weekly Teen Improv Night at ColdTowne Theater.
