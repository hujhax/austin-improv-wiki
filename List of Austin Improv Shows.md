#### Themed Troupe Shows
Often at ColdTowne, a troupe will do a themed mainstage run.  Typically, these shows are listed with the troupe page.

* [[Troupes/Precious Dads]] have presented "Dads in Bars" in January 2013 and "Home for the Holidays" in November 2013, and will present "The Barmando" in March 2014.

* [[Troupes/Stag Comedy]] has presented "Stab Comedy".
* [[Troupes/Wink Planet]] will present "Pilgrims Are From Mars" in November 2014.
