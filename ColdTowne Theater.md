Coldtowne Theater is an Austin improv theater that focuses on Chicago-style improv.

## History
The theater was founded by the troupe [[ColdTowne]] after they were displaced from New Orleans by [http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hurricane_Katrina Hurricane Katrina].
