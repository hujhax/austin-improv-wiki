**ZA** was an improv duo.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2012 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>ZA consists of two veteran improvisers exploring scenes with grounded characters in real situations.  We aspire to explore a moment in time with these characters while still leaving space for a sense of playfulness and fun that is inherent in both of our personal styles of improvising.</blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2012 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote><p>Basically we will be performing mostly monoscene, but with the possibility of break outs or moving onto something new if one scene seems like it has run its course.</p> 

<p>The biggest thing we want to focus on is the idea of keeping energy going throughout a monoscene, hopefully playing with a wide range of emotion and energy levels and making sure that we entertain ourselves and each other at least as much as we are entertaining the audience.</p></blockquote>
