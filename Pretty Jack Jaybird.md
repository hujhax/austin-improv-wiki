**Pretty Jack Jaybird** was a musical-montage improv troupe.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2010 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote><p>Pretty Jack Jaybird, featuring [[Jay Byrd]], [[Patrick Knisely]] and [[Tej Ludher]], performs what they like to call a MAMIM.</p> 

<p>A music and movement infused montage.</p> 

<p>It's an all out organic journey to absurdity with singing and dancing.</p></blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2010 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote><p>Pretty Jack Jaybird performs what they call a "Music and Movement Infused Montage."</p> 

<p>Using organic changes between scenes with improvised singing, rhythms and movement, Pretty Jack Jaybird digs into the absurd, while singing and dancing around it, in a grounded manner.</p> 

<p>They perform an alternative to the traditional improv show, as they integrate improvised music and movement without the trappings of normal improvised musicals or dances (that might have a narrative thread or completely lack any scenework).</p> 

<p>Pretty Jack Jaybird is the product of three individuals who met in an improv class at The New Movement Theater in Austin TX.</p> 

<p>They performed a show together at The Hideout Theater in Austin, TX representing The New Movement that was wildly succesful.</p> 

<p>After this show, they decided continue performing together to pursue their unique style.</p> 

<p>Collectively they have studied improv all over Austin (The Hideout Theater, The New Movement Theater, ColdTowne Theater and Merlin-Works).</p></blockquote>
