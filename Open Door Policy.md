**Open Door Policy** was an improv duo.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2011 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>Open Door Policy came about when Mike Sullivan's improv partner couldn't make it to a show and John Ratliff stepped in. The rest is history, in the sense that it has already happened. </blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2011 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>We will be doing two person improv scenes. We would like to inject silly game energy into the edits and then have grounded scenes.</blockquote>
