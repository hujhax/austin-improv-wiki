**The TV Set** is a performance slot at [[The Hideout Theatre]].

It occurs at 8pm on the third Friday of every month as part of [[The Wheel]], and specializes in TV-related shows.  Previous invitees have included [[The Professor]], *[[Totally Improvised, 90210]]*, and *[[Twilight Zoned]]*.
