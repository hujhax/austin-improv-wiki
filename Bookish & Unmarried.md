**Bookish and Unmarried** was an improv troupe.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2013 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>Each member trained at a different Austin improv school, and together their forces combined unleash a character driven narrative that progresses forward and sometimes backward.</blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2013 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>We have been coached every week by Tom Booker.  This is a long term project and we are trying to begin the process of playing live and getting shows.</blockquote>
