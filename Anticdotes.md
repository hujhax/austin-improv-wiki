**Anticdotes** was an improv troupe.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2010 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>Anticdotes is the newest improv troupe to be created out of [[Gnap! Theater Projects]]. Comprised of an all-star cast of students from [[The MerlinWorks Institute of Improvisation]], Anticdotes plays with an explosive, high-risk style that explores physical movement. </blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2010 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>Right now, we use the audience's suggestion to run a series of freezes that then inspire a montage (flowy) of scenes. 

Within the context of those scenes, anything goes (i.e. we range from 10 second scenes, to multiple scenes from the same storyline).</blockquote>
