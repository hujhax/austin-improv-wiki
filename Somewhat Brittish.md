**Somewhat Brittish** was an improv duo.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2011 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>She's Britt and he's a Brit. Together, they are Somewhat Brittish. </blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2011 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>We chat a bit on stage before we begin our set, to get some ideas. Then we begin our monoscene. </blockquote>

## History
The duo disbanded in August 2011, when the two members moved to Chicago and New Orleans.
