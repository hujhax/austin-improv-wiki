## How much discussion of controversy of TNM Ownership is appropriate?
This wiki is a tool primarily to provide information that should be accessible to everyone without antagonizing anyone unnecessarily. But the alleged misconduct by owners of TNM is directly relevant to how Fallout Theater came into existence. The theater was formed out of a strike by the TNM ATX community which shutdown the theater and created Fallout Theater.
