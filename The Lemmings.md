**The Lemmings** was an improv troupe.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2013 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>Courtney, Cortnie and Val like to agree. What if you felt strongly about every single thing that happened to you? What if you changed your mind at the drop of the hat? We'll agree hard core all the time, even if it means we follow each other off a cliff.</blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2013 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>We're into super agreeing. We agree to the point of absurdity. That is all we do.</blockquote>
