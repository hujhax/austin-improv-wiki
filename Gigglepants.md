**Gigglepants** is the college troupe for University of Texas that has been the starting point for a few improvisors around town and continues to do shows on a bi-weekly basis.

## History
Gigglepants was founded in the fall of 2003 by [[Bryan Cruz]], [[Thai Le]], and [[Tom Glinkowski]]. Alums of [[ComedySportz High School League]] in Houston, the troupe is named after their mutual coach [[Chris Craig|Chris "Chim Chim Gigglepants" Craig]].

The troupe started performing in the spring semester of 2004. The original cast also included [[David Toscano]], [[Bryan Roberts]], [[Shawn Davis]], [[Major Hoffman]], [[Brandon Stackhouse]], [[Jason Dunkelberger]], [[Sylvia Ortiz]], and [[Connor McGee]].
