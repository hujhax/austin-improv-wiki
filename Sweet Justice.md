**Sweet Justice** is an improv troupe.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2015 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>Luke and Nicole play two incredibly nice, cheery, chipper co-workers who happen to be homicide detectives that regularly get assigned to the grisliest cases. 

Kevin Miller is their no-nonsense boss who regularly doles out the assignments and has had it up to here with their unrelenting cheeriness. 

Expect games and audience interaction.</blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2015 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>We essentially do a narrative that follows the exploits of two well-meaning detectives. 

As the story evolves, members of the audience may be invited to take on certain roles and help guide where the story ends up. 

It's fun!</blockquote>
