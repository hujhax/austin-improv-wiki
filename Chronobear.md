**Chronobear** was an improv troupe.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2012 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>Chronobear is time travel in the sense that they take you to a moment in 3 guys's life and then show you the next 25 minutes. It's very funny.</blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2012 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>We do a monoscene.</blockquote>
