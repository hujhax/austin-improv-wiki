**Wombmates** is an improv troupe.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2013 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>Three guys who feel like they were separated at birth, The WombMates share a love for high energy montage based improv. Having played together in several of austin's finest troupes, they streamlined what they love about each and brought it to the Mates!</blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2013 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote><p>We are kinda figuring it out as we go but at the Spectacle that we just played (thank you VERY much) we tried this new "Monologue" format. It really worked well. At different times during scenes characters would step to center stage, a spot would close in and they would color that character for a minute or two. Especially with this group, it was a nice way to slow the pace and also get the audience way more invested in these characters.</p> 

<p>It always inspire the other 2 performers when we returned to the scene. We're really gonna work at this format more.</p></blockquote>
