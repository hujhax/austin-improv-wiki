**Ed32** was a short-form improv troupe founded by [[Andy Crouch]] and based out of UT. It was the first improv troupe for several active improvisers, including [[Michael Ferstenfeld]], [[Curtis Luciani]], [[Caitlin Sweetlamb]], and [[Valerie Ward]].

The troupe was named for founding member [[Ed Piston]], who was 32 at the time of the troupe's founding, making him at least a decade older than the other members. The name persisted after Ed left the troupe, and a troupe t-shirt included a picture of him.
