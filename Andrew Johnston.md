Andrew Johnston is an improv performer and sometimes magician. 

Andrew began studying at The Institution Theater in early 2012 in order to overcome stage fright.  Although he continues an interest in magic, studies at TIT have become his primary performing outlet. 

## Shows
* *[[Hell & Back Cabaret]]* - Magician, pre-show lobby entertainment (2013)
* *[[Mister Morbid’s Moonlight Movie Mayhem Madness Massacre]]* – Magician, “Well Dressed Homeless Man” (2013)

Andrew has played once in [[Maestro]] and once in [[Fancy Pants]].   He is a founding member of [[Candy Vampires]]
