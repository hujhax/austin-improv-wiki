**Price Check** was an improv duo.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2013 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>Price Check eschews the usual "rules" of improv by taking the two person transaction scene (a general no-no) and turning it into an entire show giving you a full immersion into the transaction experience through attention to detail and focussed listening. Transactional improv is here and Price Check to make sure you got your exact change</blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2013 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>Andrew Basile and Matt Needles seek to break improv convention by engaging in a legitimate transaction scene with all the realness and spacework change that entails.</blockquote>
