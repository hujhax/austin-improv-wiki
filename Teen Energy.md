**Teen Energy** was an improv troupe.

## Summary
### Press Blurb
Their press blurb, taken from a 2014 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>Teen Energy is a group of high school girls who are all very different from each other (you know, classic high school stereotypes--artsy, nerd, cheerleader, edgy) but who all happen to have magical powers and use them for everyday high school challenges (casting spells on boys, telekinesis to clean your room, etc.) </blockquote>

### "What's Your Deal?"
Their answer to the "What's Your Deal?" question on a 2014 application to perform at [[The Hideout Theatre]]:<blockquote>We tell a story. It takes place in a high school. There's some magic involved. </blockquote>
